# 2 Timothy 3 (LSB)
#biblestudy/devotionals/2timothy/2025

Paul wanted Timothy to remember that the sacred writings that he knew from his childhood have always helped in making him wise. And not just wise, but wise unto salvation. 
Pray attention to this word unto, which means for the purpose of. It is this wisdom that has kept Timothy and brought him thus far. 

It is this same wisdom which has kept Paul through all of the hardships that Timothy is aware of and at times has even been a first hand witness of. All of this wisdom is needed so that Timothy is able to withstand the hardships coming his way.
