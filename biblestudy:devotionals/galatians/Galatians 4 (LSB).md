# Galatians 4 (LSB)
### SONSHIP IN CHRIST

> **Gal. 4:1**   Now I say, as long as the heir is a child, he does not differ at all from a slave although he is owner of everything, **2** but he is under guardians and stewards until the date set by the father. **3** So also we, while we were children, were enslaved under the elemental things of the world.

Our guardian and stewards are not the Law. We are not enslaved by them. Yes, it is Scripture, and the Law, that has shut us up inside a boundary, but that was to keep us safe. Inside this boundary, however, we have been **enslaved** by *our own lust* (James 1:14) for and **under the elemental things of the world**. #biblestudy/discovery
So Scripture is the boundary, Law was our tutor and the things of the world is the slave master (cf. [[1 John 2 (LSB)/Essay: The New Commandment|notes on 1 John 2:15]]). 
Even though the analogy speaks of **a child**, if we were to extend this analogy and specify the details, we would not even be children *of the home owner*. We would merely be children of another family. Jesus entered this worldly house in order to save us, who were not even God's children or part of His people. Everyone who is not an Israelite did not receive the promise made to Abraham and his seed (Genesis 12). But Jesus asked His Father to adopt us so that we could be heirs with Him, on a few conditions (e.g. 1 John 4:15, 5:1, Acts 2:36-38, namely that we repent and confess Him as Lord and Savior). Only after these events, v. 6 can be read and understood. 

> **4** But when the fullness of the time came, God sent forth His Son, born of a woman, born under the Law, **5** so that He might redeem those who were under the Law, that we might receive the adoption as sons. 

It is a very intuitive analogy when God explains that He needed to go to Earth and become man in order to save them. Even in stories with similar motifs we see that a protagonist had to do just that, such as in the animated movie #tags/movies/animation *Epic* or, on a more macro scale, *John Carter*. However, in those stories the person who goes to this new realm is still the same person with the same knowledge and power. Jesus, on the other hand, came to Earth and put off His special knowledge and powers and received only these things he received from the Father at *designated* times. That is to say that at certain times there were things He could not do or know, such as the time that He was going to return to Earth (#todo/opzoeken/reference). But more than just one step *down* into our physical realm, He also stepped down into the spiritual realm by coming *under the Law*. Remember that Jesus is not only *above* the Law, but He *created* it. And now He needs to abide by every Law with the risk of—upon trespassing even the least important law by a fraction—being damned eternally in hell, together with all of His creation. Of course this risk was close infinitesimally small, but the fact that Jesus, that is God Himself, was willing to put this even as a wager, is proof of His infinite love for His creation. #biblestudy/discovery

> **6** And because you are sons, God sent forth the Spirit of His Son into our hearts, crying, “Abba! Father!” **7** Therefore you are no longer a slave, but a son; and if a son, then an heir through God.

This verse makes clear that it is the Spirit of His Son, that is the Holy Spirit, that cries out to the Lord. The Dutch Reformed and Calvinists are of the opinion that man is *totally depraved* #biblestudy/theology/calvinism/totaldepravity and cannot call out or even wish to become Christian. But the step *before* **God sent forth the Spirit** is that we had to repent. So, yes, man is totally depraved but not in such a way that even repentance is impossible. Repentance and submission to the Lord are done freely from man's own will, *after which* the Spirit calls out to the Father on behalf of us, because we cannot and do not know how to speak with the Father. In this case Jesus Christ, and His spirit, are like an advocate (cf. 1 John 2:1), just like regular people do not know how to speak in court or even how to address the Court and the judge. #biblestudy/discovery

> **Gal. 4:8**   However at that time, when you did not know God, you were slaves to those which by nature are no gods. **9** But now, having known God, or ==rather having been known by God==, how is it that you turn back again to the weak and worthless elemental things, to which you want to be enslaved all over again?

Even now, it seems that Paul cannot say with confidence that the Galatians know God. Yes, they had **been known by God**, but they themselves did not know God. As probably mentioned before #todo/opzoeken/reference the recipients of this letter were a mix of true and nominal Christians, and of the latter group there were those who were naïve and those who were deliberately abusing their freedom in Christ or even leading others astray. 

> **10** You observe days and months and seasons and years. 

What the Galatians **turned back to** was the observance of feasts. While this is not a sin in itself, not even for Christians who are under the New Covenant, it does form a problem when one follows this too strictly. Exodus #todo/opzoeken/reference says that three times a year each adult man *must* make the trip to Jerusalem to celebrate the feasts of the Lord (right name? #todo/opzoeken ) and sacrifice an animal. This rule does not apply to the believers under the New Covenant, i.e. Christians. From a logical standpoint this makes sense as well. In the New Covenant believers lived under two laws, those who lived in Israel had to obey the Mosaic Law and the Roman Law, and to both God and the government one had to pay taxes. This is why Romans 11 #todo/opzoeken/reference mentions the role of the government in the life of a Christian. But under the New Covenant new believers all around the world would be continuously arise, and it would be impossible for all of them to come to Jerusalem three times a year to sacrifice. It would also be impossible given the great number of Christians there would be in the world. Lastly, it would be impossible because the Temple would cease to exist in just a matter of years. #biblestudy/discovery 
The Judaizers, that is those false teachers who wanted to bring back Christians to the Jewish faith, thought they were right and perhaps had even found the best concession, by keeping their faith in Christ and yet not be punished by the Jews, but in a few years, at the destruction of the Temple in 66 A.D., #todo/opzoeken/reference they would see no solution of how to keep God's Law anymore and thus exercise their religion. #biblestudy/discovery
Yahweh is all-knowing and He knows how His people can worship Him in all times. He is not like Allah who through Muhammad set all kinds of rules and when Muhammad could not abide by a rule, he changed the rule, either temporarily or permanently, for instance when he was not allowed to camel or rabbit, since both are not *kosher* (I am assuming Muhammad copied the rules of halal food from the Jews), he changed to rule to making this *halal* (#todo/opzoeken/reference permissible to eat). When he was not allowed to lie, he sought a way out by lying anyway and created the rule that lying is permissible if it is done for a fellow muslim. God never needed these exceptions. The friends of Daniel (Daniel 3) stayed loyal to God and refused to bow down to Nebuchadnezzar and God showed them mercy. Daniel refused to eat food #todo/opzoeken/reference Daniel 1, and God made him thrive on only vegetables. #biblestudy/discovery

> **11** I fear for you, that perhaps I have labored over you for nothing.

Paul was not threatening his readers here, but was genuinely afraid he had **labored for nothing**, meaning that some in the churches of Galatia would ultimately not be saved. This is by interpretation that the **you** does not refer to everyone, since Paul was address only those who were reverting back to the old ways. This meant either that the people could lose their salvation or that they had never been saved. I will skip the discussion concerning the possibility of losing one's salvation #biblestudy/theology/calvinism/osas but if the second point was meant, then Paul was not speaking to *only Christians*, even though he calls them **brothers** (v. 12, also in Galatians 1:11 and elsewhere). These two facts, i.e. the usage of "you" for only a part of his readers, and calling others "brothers" when they are not Christian, Paul does in more places. People have based doctrines, however, on the theory that all recipients of his epistles were a homogenous group, i.e. they were all (un)believers,

> **17** They zealously seek you, not commendably, but they wish to shut you out so that you will zealously seek them. 

This group that propagated a different gospel (cf. Galatians 1:8f) used a tactic that is still commonly used today in Marketing. By *excluding* people, people actually *want* to become part of a group. 
**Example.** A historic example of this occurred in the time when potatoes were not commonly eaten because the people did not like them. #todo/opzoeken/reference Most likely because they were cheap and easy to plant, the government thought of suggesting these to people, but they were too bland to enjoy as food. So they thought of forbidding people from eating potatoes and only give it to the rich. What happened, was that the people went to the deliberately not guarded potato fields at night and steal potatoes, and that is how potatoes became a common food. 
**Example.** Another example of this is when Mark Zuckerberg #tags/people founded *Facebook* it was only available for people of Ivy League schools, even though much more mature social networking sites such as *Hi5* and *Hyves* already existed. It gained huge popularity because of this and has since become the biggest social network platform. #biblestudy/discovery
Perhaps Paul means the same thing here and warns the Galatians against falling in their trap. However, another interpretation is that the false teachers want to exclude the Galatians from the true believers, so that the Galatians will then follow the false teachers. This is also what every cult does (and is one of the conditions in the definition of a cult[^1]), namely restricting people from having contact with their family, especially with those who are against their cult. 

> **18** But it is good always to be zealously sought in a commendable manner, and not only when I am present with you. 

The difficulty in the interpretation of this verse is that is has the phrase **zealously sought**. In the KJV it almost seems like it says "to be zealous", which is of course a good thing. But to be *sought* is something that does not occur often. The Bible tells believers not to draw attention to themselves, accept honor or to put oneself on a pedestal. On the other hand, though, it does say to honor the elders (#todo/opzoeken/reference ) although it is clear that they should merely be revered, with deference, and not venerated (cf. Colossians 3:25, James 2:3).
Even though the false gospel-bearers were sinful, this does not take away the fact that it is good when one is zealously sought. People could spend many hours on a specific dish or on cooking themselves and become a Michelin chef, or in a sport and become an Olympic medal winner or anything else. It is their zealousness, and with that their discipline and effort, that has led them to their current state. Unfortunately this zealousness was not applied to their faith, which is more important, but zealousness in itself is not wrong. 
However, if we are speaking of a famous and honorable man, such as Alistair Begg, Paul Washer or John MacArthur #tags/people/christian#—men of God and of good reputation, as good as above reproach—these men are not in themselves zealously sought after, but their way of living and their preaching is sought. This, then, is a good thing if all glory goes to God. #biblestudy/discovery 

> **19** My children, with whom I am again in labor until Christ is ==formed== in you— 

**Christ** was not yet **formed** in them. Again, this can be interpreted as that the readers were *not* Christians yet or they were very young and immature Christians. The longer they stayed in this immature state, the longer Paul was **in labor**. For them it was even the *second* time, for Paul worked to bring them the Gospel the first time, and now he has to work **again** to undo the teachings of the Judaizers.

> **20** but I could wish to be present with you now and to change my tone, because I am perplexed about you.

Paul expresses his wish to be present with the Galatians. Teaching is always done better in person than over a distance through letters. Paul does not like to speak with the stern tone he uses in this letter, but it is because he is **perplexed**, and perhaps it is needed to wake them up. 

> ### AN ALLEGORY OF TWO COVENANTS
> 
> **Gal. 4:21**   Tell me, you who want to be under law, do you not listen to the Law? **22** For it is written that Abraham had two sons, one by the servant-woman and one by the free woman. **23** But the son by the servant-woman had been born according to the flesh, while the son by the free woman through the promise. **24** This is spoken with allegory, for these women are two covenants: one from Mount Sinai bearing children into slavery; she is Hagar. **25** Now this Hagar is Mount Sinai in Arabia and corresponds to the present Jerusalem, for she is in slavery with her children.

People who are reading this for the first time—and especially Jews—will be shocked to see that instead of an expected exposition of two opposites—with Mount Sinai on the one side and Mount Zion, which is Jerusalem, on the other side—*both* mountains are symbolic for slavery. So both Arabs and Jews are enslaved (as well as the rest of the world) and have no access to God. Of the two **women**, one is Sarah—mother of all Israelites (and thus Jews)—and since the Jews are born from her they will expect that they are not born into slavery and have free access to the Father. Yet the reverse is true, for though they did receive the promise of God, they themselves did not abide by the *conditions* of the promise and they *chose* to forfeit that promise. #biblestudy/discovery

> **26** But the Jerusalem above is free; she is our mother. 

Only those who belong to *Heaven* are free, not those born by blood to Sarah, but those who are under the blood of Jesus Christ. 

> **27** For it is written,
> 	“REJOICE, BARREN WOMAN WHO DOES NOT GIVE BIRTH;
> 	BREAK FORTH AND SHOUT, YOU WHO ARE NOT IN LABOR;
> 	FOR MORE NUMEROUS ARE THE CHILDREN OF THE DESOLATE ONE
> 	THAN OF THE ONE WHO HAS A HUSBAND.”
> **28** And you brothers, in accordance with Isaac, are children of promise. 

Note that all unbelievers are children of the servant-woman, not only the Ishmaelites. At the same time, any person, regardless of his heritage, God has named **children of promise.**

> **29** But as at that time he who was born according to the flesh was persecuting him *who was born* according to the Spirit, so it is now also. 

Ishmael persecuted Isaac #todo/opzoeken/reference (to be precise it was Rahab who taunted Sarah), and later on the Ishmaelites bought and sold Joseph as slave. Many centuries later the Arabs would persecute Jews in the time of Muhammad all the way until now, even though, now, Israel can protect itself as a country. But the ones who are **born according to the Spirit** are the Christians, since Pentecost, and so after Pentecost it was the Christians who were persecuted by the Jews. The Jews showed the fulfilment of this passage by their own actions.

> **30** But what does the Scripture say?
> 	“CAST OUT THE SERVANT-WOMAN AND HER SON,
> 	FOR THE SON OF THE SERVANT-WOMAN SHALL NOT BE AN HEIR WITH THE SON OF THE FREE WOMAN.”

This command applies to the Jewish nation first of all (outside of the fact that it was first mean for Sarah to cast out Rahab and Ishmael) to cast out the Arabs, spiritually, for they will not have an inheritance with the people of God, even though many Muslims think so. But in second, and much more important, instance, this applies to everyone who does not accept Jesus Christ as their Lord and Savior. Everyone who does not have Jesus as Lord, has a different lord #todo/opzoeken/reference (you will always serve a lord) and everyone who has a lord is not free. Only servants of the Lord Jesus Christ are actually free in Christ. #biblestudy/discovery
