# Matthew 16 (LSB)

“From that time Jesus began to show His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem, and suffer many things from the elders and chief priests and scribes, and be killed, and be raised up on the third day. And Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him, saying, “God forbid it, Lord! This shall never happen to You.” But He turned and said to Peter, “Get behind Me, Satan! You are a stumbling block to Me; for you are not setting your mind on God’s interests, but man’s.” Then Jesus said to His disciples, “If anyone wishes to come after Me, let him deny himself, and take up his cross and follow Me. For whoever wishes to save his life will lose it; but whoever loses his life for My sake will find it.”

There are many reasons why Peter could have stopped Jesus from going this path of death. Obvious reasons would be that he loved Him and did not want to lose Him. Also, Jesus was stronger than everything else and could destroy His enemies and did not need to die. 
There is another reason, but it is less likely that Peter was aware of this, if it is true, and that is that whatever Jesus does, His disciples must do even more. By argument of the greater and the lesser, #todo/opzoeken if Jesus was going to die, and even suffer before that, His disciples would suffer even more. 

In reality no one—not His disciples nor anyone else in this world—has suffered as much as Jesus has. Not only the crucifixion was gruesome (for there were deaths of others, among whom His disciples, which were equally painful) but the wrath of God poured on Him directly and the absence of His Father were the most painful events in history. 
However, Peter could not have known this, but he could have deducted by reason that his suffering  would be at least that of Jesus. #biblestudy/discovery
Either way, Peter was setting his mind on his own interests. The problem was not selfishness. Even if he had set his mind on the interests of others, that is **of man**, he would still be just as wrong. The interest of God is  what is most important. 
As a matter of fact, the Left is committing this same sin. Even when the world is altruistic—which it is almost never—it has the interest of *man* in mind, putting him first instead of God.
