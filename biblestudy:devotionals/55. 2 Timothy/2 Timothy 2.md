# 2 Timothy 2 
#biblestudy/devotionals/2timothy/2025

Note that the phrase "**is to**" indicates an imperative. It is not advisory, but a command. 

Conversely, or reversely? Everyone who does not depart from wickedness is not to name the Name of the Lord.
Whoever calls Yahweh his God, admits that he is his worshipper. If he calls Him Lord or Master, he is His slave. If he calls God Father, he is His son. No worshipper, servant or son will do anything that is wicked to his respective god, master or father. If he does, he is not a worshipper, servant or son. 


There is general consensus among Christians about the order of the chain of salvation. The Christian must first realize that he is sinful, then repent of his sins, commit his life to Christ, only after which he receives the Holy Spirit. The discussion between Christians is when the Holy Spirit is involved. The doctrine of Total Depravity states that a person is not able to do any of the three steps, and cannot even realize he is sinful unless the Holy Spirit works through him. This automatically leads to God's provision, for it is God Who sends the Holy Spirit to whom He chooses.
