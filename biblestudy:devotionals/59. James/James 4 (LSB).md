No one can tame the tongue. Whereas I have mentioned in verses... #todo/opzoeken/reference that most are not able to control their thoughts, here it is explicitly said that **no one can tame the tongue**, and if no one can tame that, most certainly no one can tame the mind and the thoughts that go before it, unless one would claim that the mouth has a will of its own. 
The tongue is personified here as **a restless evil and full of poison**. If all the animals and reptiles can be tamed, or at least locked up or killed, though some reptiles have poison as well, why can the tongue not be tamed? The answer is that though reptiles are poisonous, they are always so and have no evil intentions. The tongue however, as James writes, can bless and curse. It can pretend it is your friend and the moment you turn around it can stab you in the back. If an animal were ever so cunning, like *Puss in Boots* in the movie  #tags/movies/animation *Shrek*, man would be much less capable of taming that animal. See for example horses who have been tamed. The greatest accidents occur when people think horses are tamed but then go wild again. E.g. The horse whisperer. 
Or robots who turned against man, such as in iRobot.
