# 1 Peter 5 (LSB)

> **1** Therefore, I exhort the elders among you, as *your* fellow elder and witness of the sufferings of Christ, and a partaker also of the glory that is to be revealed,

Peter speaks with authority to **exhort** the elders of these churches, because he is an **elder** as well, and to the Christians, because he also is **a partaker [...] of the glory**, but even more, which no one other than the other apostles can boast of, he is a **witness of the sufferings of Christ**. 

> **2** shepherd the flock of God among you, overseeing not under compulsion, but willingly, according to God; and not for dishonest gain, but with eagerness;

Shepherding a flock would mean the flock is healthy and has good produce, whether that be meat, milk, wool or offspring. In the same way Christians have to produce fruit, which comes first of all because of the Holy Spirit, but aside from that also from the guidance the Christian receives from his elders. 
It is amazing to see how much of a difference the right guidance can make in the life of a young) Christian. I have known Christians who might have not been Christians or even might have become psychopaths, if it were not for the influence of a godly teacher in their life. 
An elder is not sure to gain anything material by it, he will only receive honor from God. That is why **eagerness** must be present, and not gree or another kind of motivation. 

> **3** nor yet as lording it over those allotted to you, but being ==examples== to the flock. **4** And when the Chief Shepherd appears, you will ==receive== the unfading crown of glory. **5** You younger men, likewise, be subject to *your* elders. And all of you, clothe yourselves with humility toward one another, for GOD IS OPPOSED TO THE ==PROUD==, BUT GIVES GRACE TO THE HUMBLE.

Two commands are given here by Peter, just like he has given two commands to the leader of the family, the husband, and the follower, the wife. The commands are almost similar. The leader has to take care of the follower in a humble way, so that the follower does not **subject** himself out of **compulsion, but willingly**. This form of authority is an example to more than just these two areas and should be practiced in all areas, even in the army, where a sergeant or other type of leader would be strict, but would give his life for his men any time. Ultimately, God is the leader Who first gave this example and showed his humility through His patience with His people. 
The quote mentions *pride*, but the aforementioned aspect of **lording it over** others is emphasized here. 

> **1Peter 5:6**   Therefore humble yourselves under the mighty hand of God, that He may exalt you at the proper time, **7** CASTING ALL YOUR ANXIETY ON HIM, because He cares for you. 

Just like we have to submit to authority in this world, so spiritually we must **humble [ourselves] under the mighty hand of God**. The husband will exalt his wife, is she is pleasing in his eyes, and so will the employer, how much more will God not praise His servants? Like a good shepherd, He wants us to **cast [our] anxiety on Him**, for He does not want us to be afraid (cf. Joshua 1:8), **because He cares for** us, but also because this fear could harm us, for it distracts us and makes us less **watchful**.

> **8** Be of sober *spirit*, be watchful. Your adversary, the devil, prowls around like a roaring lion, seeking someone to devour. 

Again, our spirit needs to be **sober** (cf. 1 Peter 1: #todo/opzoeken/reference ), meaning that there should be no worries or other distracting things in our thoughts. The devil is always around us, waiting for the right time. Where the Bible speaks of the demon who was cast out and then brought seven other demons with him to enter the clean house, the truth is that the Satan had been watching that house all the time, waiting for the right moment to enter the house again. 

> **9** But resist him, firm in the faith, knowing that the same experiences of suffering are being accomplished among your brethren who are in the world.

As with all things, it is easier to **resist [the devil]** when we do it together with others. Even in Peter's day Christians were persecuted all around **the world**. Peter likely means that our Christian **brethren** lived *in* the world, wanting to spread the Gospel there *to* the world and as a result being persecuted *by* the world, but it is a fact that even in those days Christians were persecuted everywhere where Christians were present, all the way from Rome to Turkey, and later on even in India. 
**Example.** At the moment of writing my wife has a rare disease called *Topical Steroid Withdrawal Syndome* (TSW) for which no cure exists, and most doctors are ignorant about this or deny its presence. This disease expresses itself through eczema-like symptoms, but has much more symptoms than just the dryness of the skin[^1]. There are many people in the word though, with incurable forms of skin disease, and when all medical care and medine fail, they go to communities, such as the Carnivore-community, where many have shown progress and even complete healing by going on the carnivore diet. TSW itself also has a community where the people support each other. All of this helps in enduring the burden of carrying the illness and the slightly more difficult way of eating. 

> **10** And after you have suffered for a little while, the God of all grace, who called you to His eternal glory in Christ, will Himself restore, strengthen, confirm, *and* ground you. **11** To Him *be* might forever and ever. Amen.

Even better than a mere community is the hope and expectation we have that **God [...] will Himself [...] restore** us and we will be with Him. 

> **1Peter 5:12**   Through Silvanus, our faithful brother as I regard *him*, I have written to you briefly, exhorting and bearing witness that this is the true grace of God. Stand firm in it! **13** She who is in Babylon, chosen together with you, sends you greetings, and *so does* my son, Mark. **14** Greet one another with a kiss of love.

>  Peace be to you all who are in Christ.
