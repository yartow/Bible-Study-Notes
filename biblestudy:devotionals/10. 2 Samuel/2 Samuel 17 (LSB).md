# 2 Samuel 17 (LSB)
> ### Absalom Calls for Hushai’s Counsel
> **2Sam. 17:1**   Then Ahithophel said to Absalom, “Please let me choose 12,000 men that I may arise and pursue David tonight. **[^2]** “[^a]And I will come upon him while he is weary with *his* hands falling limp and throw him into *utter* fright, so that all the people who are with him will flee. Then [^b]I will strike down the king alone, **[^3]** and I will cause all the people to return to you. [^1]The return of everyone depends on the man you seek; *then* all the people will be at [^a]peace.” **[^4]** And the word was right in the eyes of Absalom and all the elders of Israel.
> 
> **2Sam. 17:5**   Then Absalom said, “Call now [^a]Hushai the Archite also, and let us hear what is also in his mouth.” **[^6]** Then Hushai came to Absalom. And Absalom spoke to him, saying, “Ahithophel has spoken [^1]thus. Shall we [^2]carry out his word? If not, you speak.” **[^7]** And Hushai said to Absalom, “[^a]This time the counsel that Ahithophel has counseled is not good.” **[^8]** Then Hushai said, “You know your father and his men, that they are mighty men and they are bitter of soul, [^a]like a bear robbed of her cubs in the field. And your father is a man of war, and will not spend the night with the people. **[^9]** “Behold, he has now hidden himself in one of the [^1]caves or in another place; and it will be [^2]when he falls on them at the first *attack*, that whoever hears *it* will say, ‘There has been a slaughter among the people who follow Absalom.’ **[^10]** “And even the one who is a man of valor, whose heart is like the heart of a lion, [^a]will utterly melt; for all Israel knows that your father is a mighty man and those who are with him are men of valor. **[^11]** “But I counsel that all Israel be utterly gathered to you, [^a]from Dan even to Beersheba, [^b]as the sand that is by the sea in abundance, and that [^1]you personally go into battle. **[^12]** “So we shall come to him in one of the places where he can be found, and we will set down on him [^a]as the dew falls on the ground; and of him and of all the men who are with him, not even one will be left. **[^13]** “If he withdraws into a city, then all Israel shall carry ropes to that city, and we will [^a]drag it into the [^1]valley until not even a small stone is found there.” **[^14]** Then Absalom and all the men of Israel said, “The counsel of Hushai the Archite is better than the counsel of Ahithophel.” For [^a]Yahweh had [^1]ordained to thwart the good counsel of Ahithophel, so that Yahweh might bring calamity on Absalom.
> 
> ### Hushai Informs David
> **2Sam. 17:15**   Then [^a]Hushai said to Zadok and to Abiathar the priests, “[^1]This is what Ahithophel counseled Absalom and the elders of Israel, and [^2]this is what I have counseled. **[^16]** “So now, send quickly and inform David, saying, ‘[^a]Do not spend the night at the fords of the wilderness, but by all means cross over, lest the king and all the people who are with him be swallowed up.’” **[^17]** [^a]Now Jonathan and Ahimaaz were staying at [^b]En-rogel, and a servant-woman would go and inform them, and they would go and inform King David, for they could not be seen entering the city. **[^18]** But a boy did see them and informed Absalom; so the two of them went quickly and came to the house of a man [^a]in Bahurim, who had a well in his courtyard, and they went down [^1]into it. **[^19]** And [^a]the woman [^1]took a covering and spread it over the well’s mouth and scattered crushed grain on it, so that nothing was known. **[^20]** Then Absalom’s servants came to the woman at the house and said, “Where are Ahimaaz and Jonathan?” And [^a]the woman said to them, “They have crossed the brook of water.” And when they searched and could not find *them*, they returned to Jerusalem.
> 
> **2Sam. 17:21**   Now it happened after they went away, that they came up out of the well and went and informed King David; and they said to David, “[^a]Arise and cross over the water quickly for thus Ahithophel has counseled against you.” **[^22]** Then David and all the people who *were* with him arose and crossed the Jordan; and by [^1]dawn not even one remained who had not crossed the Jordan.
> 
> **2Sam. 17:23**   Now Ahithophel saw that his counsel was not [^1]followed. So he [^2]saddled *his* donkey and arose and went to his home, to [^a]his city, and [^3b]set his house in order, and [^c]strangled himself; thus he died and was buried in the grave of his father.
> 
> **2Sam. 17:24**   Now David came to [^a]Mahanaim. And Absalom crossed the Jordan, he and all the men of Israel with him. **[^25]** And Absalom set [^a]Amasa over the army in place of Joab. Now Amasa was the son of a man whose name was [^1]Ithra the Israelite, who went in to Abigail the daughter of [^b]Nahash, sister of Zeruiah, Joab’s mother. **[^26]** And Israel and Absalom camped in the land of Gilead.
> 
> **2Sam. 17:27**   Now it happened that when David had come to Mahanaim, Shobi [^a]the son of Nahash from [^b]Rabbah of the sons of Ammon, [^c]Machir the son of Ammiel from Lo-debar, and [^d]Barzillai the Gileadite from Rogelim, **[^28]** brought [^a]beds, basins, pottery, wheat, barley, flour, roasted *grain*, beans, lentils, roasted *seeds*, **[^29]** honey, curds, sheep, and cheese of the herd, for David and for the people who *were* with him, [^a]to eat; for they said, “The people are hungry and weary and thirsty in the wilderness.”
