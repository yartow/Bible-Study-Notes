> ### David Flees Jerusalem
> **2Sam. 15:13**   Then an informant came to David, saying, “The hearts of the men of Israel have followed Absalom.” **14** So David said to all his servants who were with him at Jerusalem, “Arise and let us flee, for *otherwise* there will be no escape for us from Absalom. Go in haste, lest he overtake us hastily and drive calamity on us and strike the city with the edge of the sword.” 

David escapes the city out of an act of love and care for the people. He does not want the fight with his son's troops to take place within the city nor does he want the people of the city who stood behind him to be killed because they protected him.
