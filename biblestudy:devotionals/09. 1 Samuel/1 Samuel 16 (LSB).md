> ### David Becomes Saul’s Armor Bearer
> **1Sam. 16:14**   [^a]Now the Spirit of Yahweh departed from Saul, and [^b]an evil spirit from Yahweh terrorized him. **[^15]** Saul’s servants then said to him, “Behold now, an evil spirit from God is terrorizing you. **[^16]** “Let our lord now speak to your servants who are before you. Let them seek a man who is a skillful musician on the harp; and it shall be that when the evil spirit from God is on you, [^a]he shall play *the harp* with his hand, and you will be well.” **[^17]** So Saul said to his servants, “Provide for me now a man who can play well and bring *him* to me.” **[^18]** Then one of the young men answered and said, “Behold, I have seen a son of Jesse the Bethlehemite who is a skillful musician, [^a]a mighty man of valor, a warrior, one who is understanding in speech, and man of fine form; and [^b]Yahweh is with him.” 

Of all the Israelites, David was not the only one who could **play the harp**, but God had led Saul's servants in such a way that they found David. Though nothing is written of David's achievements at this time, he already was *known* as **a mighty man of valor [and] a warrior**. His experience was not only with bears and lions, but also in the battlefield. But, perhaps due to his age or size—he may have been about sixteen years of age and not very tall—but also due to his talent with the sling, it was possible that he was positioned in the distant infantry #questions/language/english/spelling troop, which shot arrows and slings and had there shown his talents. 
It is very strange that nothing was said of these activities, so all of these are speculation. The only thing that is mentioned about David is that he was a shepherd, one of his skills which is not even mentioned here. 
Not all of David's brothers engaged in every war (cf. 1 Samuel 17:13) and some of them possibly had to work at their farm to keep the business going, especially since their father was already old. See notes on [[1 Samuel 17 (LSB)|1 Samuel 17:12]].

**skillful musician.** One's talents are used by God, even if it is seemingly useless, such as being able to play an instrument during war.
