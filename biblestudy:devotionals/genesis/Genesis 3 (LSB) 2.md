> **Gen. 3:16**   To the woman He said, 
> 	“I will greatly multiply 
> 	Your pain and [^1]conception, 
> 	In pain you will [^2a]bear children; 
> 	Your desire will be for your husband, 
> 	And [^b]he will rule over you.”

The consequences of this curse over the woman—and subsequently all women—is unfathomable. The pain women would have in paradise while giving birth to children would have been negligible, but now it has been greatly multiplied. 
Notice how the LSB translation distinguishes between *conception* and the *bearing of children*, while in other translations it would say something like childbearing and bringing forth children. It is hard to think why **conception** would be painful. Of course, the first time a woman has intercourse would hurt, but that would not necessarily be the moment she conceives, nor would it apply to subsequent conceptions. Mostly likely *childbirth* is the right translation for this word. The phrase **bear children** would then refer to the bearing of children in the womb, that is the nine months that women are pregnant. Perhaps that is the right translation, although these nine months only get painful in the last few months and especially the very last month. 
The next thing we should take note of is the **desire** the woman has for her **husband**.
