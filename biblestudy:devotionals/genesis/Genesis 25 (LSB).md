### The Death of Abraham
> **Gen. 25:1**   Now Abraham took another wife, 1whose name was Keturah. **2** aAnd she bore to him Zimran and Jokshan and Medan and Midian and Ishbak and Shuah. **3** Now Jokshan 1was the father of Sheba and Dedan. All these*were* the sons of Keturah. **5** aAnd Abraham gave all that he had to Isaac; 

Abraham's inheritance was split over seven sons, of which Isaac, naturally, received all (v.5), because he was the son by his most beloved wife, and because the other sons were not sons of God's promise. The other sons did not receive an inheritance but rather *gifts* only, although a gift from Abraham's would entail much. One of his sons, Midian, would become the father of the Midianites, the other ones are less well-known.  Also, the other sons were probably also not recognized as his own sons #biblestudy/questions for even though Genesis 25:1 says that Abraham took a *wife*, v.6 of this chapter and 1 Chronicles 1:32 mention Ketura as a *concubine*.

> **6** but to the sons of the concubines which Abraham had, Abraham gave gifts while he was still living, and he asent them away from his son Isaac eastward, to the land of the east.

This verse mentions a plural form of the word *concubine*, indicating that there were more wives than just Ketura. It could be that Hagar was included in this denominator, however, Ishmael's sons ruled over the Arabian peninsula/island which is now called Saudi-Arabia, and is in the *southeast* of Israel.
