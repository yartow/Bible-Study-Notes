> **Gen. 24:52**   Now it happened that when Abraham’s servant heard their words, he [^a]bowed himself to the ground [^1]before Yahweh. **[^53]** The servant brought out [^a]articles of silver and articles of gold, and garments, and gave them to Rebekah; he also gave precious things to her brother and to her mother. 

#tags/culture/ane Unfamiliar to Westerners, but the bridal payment (in Genesis 34:12 (Hebrew: *mohar*), the same root-word is translated *dowry* in Exodus 22:16) needs to be paid to her parents. There are several reasons for this. The parents had raised her and would not get anything in return. There was no more help in the household, for example, and with this money they could hire people. There could be many more reasons, some of which could be wrong, but one reason I heard was that if the man were to leave his wife, or sent her away, she would still have this price, with which she could support herself temporarily. I have heard that in the day and age #questions/language/english/spelling tegenwoordig, the engagement and wedding ring suffices for this purpose (even though many women do not even know).
