This is more than they could ever eat, about 21 liters of flour. It is custom to provide more to guests than they can eat, but this was too abundant. Perhaps he wanted to give them more for on their way. This would contradict the theory that Abraham knew that among these three men God was present. However, it was not until v.14 that Abraham knew that it was God Himself.
