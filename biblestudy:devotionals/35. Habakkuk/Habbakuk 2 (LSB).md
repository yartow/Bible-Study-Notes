> **Hab. 2:4**   “Behold, as for the proud one, 
> 	His soul is not right within him; 
> 	But the righteous will live by his faith.

Quoted in Galatians 3:11, [[Romans 1 (ESV)|Romans 1:17]], Hebrews 10:38 for three different purposes: 
In Romans the purpose is to reveal God's *righteousness*—He does not treat people by appearance, nation, ethnicity or by what they have been bestowed with (e.g. talents), but only looks at each person's faith. In Hebrews the purpose is that people continue believing and do not falter in their faith. In Galatians 3:11 the purpose is to distinguish between works and faith. Here, the purpose is to indicate that the unrighteous will not live, but the righteous will. Because Habbakuk does not want to give the idea that people can be righteous out of their own power, he added **will live by his faith**. 
These four usages do not contradict each other, but rather show the applicability in life. #biblestudy/memorization/nathan/todo #biblestudy/discovery
