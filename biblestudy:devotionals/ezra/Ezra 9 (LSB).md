Note the metaphor that Ezra uses. The **escaped remnant** is like a group of people who managed to escape from a camp or prison, and although God has always been gracious, the **grace** meant in this verse is described as if it were a ray of sunshine breaking through the clouds **for a brief moment**.

A peg could also function as a wedge, breaking or holding open the space it is in—and a peg is not much of a wedge, but God's grace is always enough for us (2 Corinthians 12:9). 
Ezra sees God as righteous and thankful for the little bit of **reviving in [their] slavery**.
