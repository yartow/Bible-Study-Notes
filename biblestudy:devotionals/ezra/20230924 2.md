Lucas 15:11-32

Jesus spends a lot of time with the poor, widows etc. but also has meetings with the Pharisees . The story of the prodigal son is in the middle, and it's important, because both peoples appear there, in the form of the eldest and youngest son. 

This parable: i want to talk about the youngest son first. we usually identify with him. For he lost everything he had. He was so hungry he would've even eaten the pigs' food. But still, it even took time before he was ready to repent. 
He wasn't worth it, but when he was dead, his father made him alive. 

The eldest son: Jesus thought most of the Pharisees when telling this part. 
The father was still so lovingly towards the son in verses 31-32. 
Christians, people who live with the father, who have been Christians for many years, but who are still complaining. Are we not people who have much knowledge and have experienced a lot? Is our attitude correct? 

First of all, pride. It might sound very spiritual, but the oldest son has a problem with his pride. 
The second problem is the identity. Who are you? A child of God. The eldest son knows he is a son of the father, but he is also holding on to other things, namely to his pains. As he mentions in v.29, he is jealous. 

In our walk with God, it's not about ourselves anymore, and not abou tthe things we've done for God. Those things we need to put down, that's the Christian life.

Philippians 3:7-16. 
Let us be one, and walk in this way. 

Short summary of this sermon. 

If you walk the way of God, you will die to yourself. 
You will not look like your old self anymore, nothing from your old life. 
If you still look like yourself of a year ago, then perhaps ... 
If you want to be like God, it means you need to let things go, even your friends, your pain and your past. Cos if you don't do that, you don't allow God to work in you. 

God works through brokenness, and makes things new every day.
