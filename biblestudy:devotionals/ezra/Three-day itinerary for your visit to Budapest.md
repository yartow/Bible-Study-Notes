# Three-day itinerary for your visit to Budapest:

**Day 1: Exploring Buda Castle District**
* 10 AM: ~Start at Buda Castle. Enjoy the panoramic view of the city from Fisherman's Bastion.~
* 11:30 AM: Explore Matthias Church nearby.
* 1 PM: Lunch at Ruszwurm Confectionery, known for its traditional Hungarian pastries.
* 2 PM: Visit the Hungarian National Gallery inside Buda Castle (entrance fee around 3000 HUF).
* 4 PM: ~Walk down Castle Hill and cross the Chain Bridge.~
* 5 PM: ~Explore St. Stephen's Basilica.~
* 6:30 PM: Dinner at Kiosk Budapest with a view of the Danube.

**Day 2: Pest Side & Thermal Baths**
* 10 AM: Visit the Hungarian Parliament Building (book tickets in advance, entrance fee around 6000 HUF).
* 11:30 AM: Walk along the Danube Promenade to Shoes on the Danube Bank memorial.
* 1 PM: Lunch at Rosenstein Restaurant, known for Hungarian-Jewish cuisine.
* 2:30 PM: Explore the Great Market Hall.
* 4 PM: Relax at Széchenyi Thermal Bath (entrance fee around 6600 HUF).
* 7 PM: Dinner at Mák Bistro for contemporary Hungarian cuisine.

**Day 3: Cultural Sites & Ruin Bars**
* 10 AM: Visit the House of Terror Museum (entrance fee around 3000 HUF).
* 11:30 AM: Explore Heroes' Square and City Park.
* 1 PM: Lunch at Kőleves Vendéglő, a charming restaurant.
* 3 PM: Visit the Museum of Fine Arts (entrance fee around 3200 HUF).
* 5 PM: Stroll along Andrassy Avenue.
* 7 PM: Dinner at Szimpla Kert, the iconic ruin bar.

**Getting Around**: Use Budapest's efficient public transport system. Purchase a 72-hour travel pass for unlimited rides on trams, buses, and the metro.

Please note that entrance fees and restaurant prices may vary based on the time of your visit, and it's recommended to check the latest information before you go. Also, make sure to make any necessary reservations in advance, especially for popular attractions. Enjoy your trip to Budapest!
