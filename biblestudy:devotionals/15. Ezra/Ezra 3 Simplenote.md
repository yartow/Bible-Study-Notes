Ezra 3 (LSB)
> From the first day of the seventh month they began to offer burnt offerings to Yahweh, but the foundation of the temple of Yahweh had not been laid. Then they gave money to the masons and carpenters, and food, drink, and oil to the Sidonians and to the Tyrians, to bring cedar wood from Lebanon to the sea at Joppa, according to the permission they had from Cyrus king of Persia.

Ezra 3:6-7 LSB

This seems to be a model of how we need to do things for God. In the first place God's need to worshipped. Sacrifices and offers need to be made, not only for the *symbolic* atonement—for animal sacrifices will never atone for the sins of man (cf. Hebrews #todo/opzoeken/reference )—but also for the worship of God. 
What is *worship* then? It is everything that pleases God. And obeying God's Law is what pleases Him. For this reason of obedience—and to commemorate the various events which the feasts refer to, such as the Exodus—we need to sacrifice daily, at the right time, and even add additional sacrifices to that.
The sacrifices in our current *dispensation*, if you will, are not animal sacrifices but these are our bodily sacrifices (cf. Romans 12:1). We keep our body, mind and soul pure (cf. 1 Corinthians 6 #todo/opzoeken/reference , John 15, 1 John 3:3) and we cut sin out of our lives, we prune it and we consider it dead (cf. Galatians 2:20).
This is what the Jews did, starting from the seventh month of their calendar year (which is approximately in November #todo/opzoeken) . 

In the second place, when we do things for God we need to do this with the right materials, and for this we need to  obtain *approval* of the building materials. First, note that materials *must be* available. There are many kinds of trees, but only the best ones, that is the cedars, are suited for the House of God. Second, note that these cedars happen to be in the possession of the *same king* who set the Israelites free to go back to their home country. The king then affirmed the right for them to take whatever they needed for the building of the Temple.
In the same way the heavenly King, Jesus Christ, set us free so that we could return to our *heavenly* home, and He owns everything in this world and permits us to use everything we need to build His house. Contrary to the Jews, we do *not* need to build His heavenly house, for Jesus is building this Himself for His bride. 
However, we build our own symbolic houses in heaven by providing the materials which we gather here on Earth (cf. 1 Corinthians 3:12ff). At the same time we are building His Kingdom in Earth as well, and this is present everywhere where His will is being done, and so we teaching, reproof, correct and train people around us so that wherever we are, God's Kingdom is built, and where we leave, others will keep on building this Kingdom.
Jesus has given us this permit through His spoken verdict of authority (Matthew 28:18-20).
