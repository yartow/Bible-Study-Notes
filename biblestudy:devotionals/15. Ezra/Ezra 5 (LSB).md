The **province beyond the River** is most likely Israel which is across the Jordan River, as seen from the East. Ezra was writing these chapters from a Persian point of view. As Christians we should comply with the requests of our government and God will reward us. > **5** But athe eye of their God was on the elders of the Jews, and they did not stop them until a report could come to Darius, and then a document be returned concerning this.

This part shows the objectiveness of the governor, who seemed to be righteous and merciful and let the Israelites continue their work. This also shows that we should not work too strictly by the Law and sometimes give people the benefit of the doubt.
