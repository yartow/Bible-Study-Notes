In the second place, when we do things for God we need to do this with the right materials, and for this we need to  obtain *approval* of the building materials. ### The Foundation of the Temple of Yahweh
> **Ezra 3:8**   Now in the second year of their coming to the house of God at Jerusalem in the second month, aZerubbabel the son of Shealtiel and Jeshua the son of Jozadak and the rest of their brothers the priests and the Levites, and all who came from the captivity to Jerusalem, began*the work* and bappointed the Levites from twenty years and older to direct the work of the house of Yahweh. 

We are now in the second year counting from *their arrival* at **the house of God at Jerusalem**, which took place a few months after Cyrus had given the Israelites permission to go back, which happened in the first year of his reign. We need to realize that all of these events took time, perhaps months, before there was any outcome. Cyrus came to power and it may have taken months before he put out the decree in the first chapter. The decree itself needed to be spread all across the Persian Empire, which definitely took multiple months. The travel from Susa to Jerusalem took at least three months, and could have been faster for messengers on horses or camels, but from Susa all the way across Iraq and Iran would have taken much more time. The Israelites were allowed to leave, but possibly needed months to gather all their stuff and collect donations from their fellow Israelites. Carts needed to be arranged—and perhaps *built* as well—to transport all the vessels of the Temple and, not to forget, all acquiring and the administration thereof needed to be done as well, for not one vessel was to be lost. 
Then, of course, the travel to Jerusalem would have taken a few months; settling, building houses for their families would have taken another few months. It would seem from Haggai 2 #todo/opzoeken/reference  that that was their preference and that that was in their hearts. Perhaps *subconsciously* they would have spent more time and more on their own homes than on the building of the temple. The necessary provisions for their own families were allowed (if I may speak for God) but the Israelites had the tendency to go beyond that and had done so as soon as they could find an excuse for this (see this [article](https://www.gotquestions.org/rebuilding-the-temple.html) for more background information). 
In all, a year and two months have passed (#todo/opzoeken the second year, or actually one whole year?) and only now they **began the work**. The encouragement we can get from this is that some things just need to happen and we need to wait for God's timing for Him to intervene and be patient *even after this* because things need time to progress. At the same time, though, we need to be efficient and cast out all things that delay the progress that depend on us.
