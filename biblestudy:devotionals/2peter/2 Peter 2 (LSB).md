# 2 Peter 2 (LSB) 
> ### The Rise of False Prophets
> **2Pet. 2:1**   But false prophets also arose among the people, just as there will also be false teachers among you, who will secretly introduce destructive heresies, even denying the Master who bought them, bringing swift destruction upon themselves.

Not only in Judaism, but also in many other religions, there were false prophets. We know about this when we look at the story of Moses, at the royal court of the Pharaoh, who had two magicians. They could be compared with prophets, false prophets in this case. In other religions, such as the Babylonian one, we see there were hundreds, if not thousands, of false prophets who advised the king (Nebuchadnezzar) in all kinds of senses in the Book of Daniel. In Israel as well, there were prophets who worshipped whatever the king wanted them to worship. Even the king himself, in this case Ahab, did not trust his own prophets. He requested a prophet who would not always say what he wanted to hear. So he requested for the prophet Micah, because he would always say something the contrary of what Ahab wanted. This, by the way, is a hallmark of a true prophet.
Outside of the religious context there are also false prophets, but because they do not call themselves prophets, they are mentioned here as **teachers**.  These are present in every age and context. They are present even in universities, in Healthcare, in the media and pretending as life coaches, among others. 
About **bought**, see notes on v. 20. 

> **2** And many will follow their sensuality, and because of them the way of the truth will be maligned.

The false teachers and prophets are always known for a couple of things: their greed for money or their lust, at many times both. This is why they bring **destruction upon themselves**. The word **their** in this verse refers to those who *follow* these false teachers, for they have a **sensuality** of *themselves* and because of this, they follow the false teacher that lures them with this. A deceiver can only lure with that which the deceived one desires. It is in this way that Adam and Eve were deceived, because they had more eye for having power and being great, than for the obedience to God.
Many false teachers have been exposed and they have all become wealthy by the donations of their church members or have had relations with many women. Some are still professing their job as "pastor", such as Creflo Dollar, Joyce Meyer and Joel Osteen #tags/people/christian/fake , some of whom have their own private jets and (multiple) million-dollar residents[^1]. The people in these churches, however, stay there and keep donating because they believe they too will become prosperous. 

> **3** And in *their* greed they will exploit you with false words, their judgment from long ago is not idle, and their destruction is not asleep.

God has not forgotten about the deeds of these false teachers. They *will* be judged and punished, either in this life or in the next. This is something we should never forget. 

> **2Pet. 2:4**   For if God did not spare angels who sinned, but cast them into the pit and delivered them to chains of darkness, being kept for judgment; **5** and did not spare the ancient world, but preserved Noah, a preacher of righteousness, with seven others, when He brought a flood upon the world of the ungodly; **6** and *if* He condemned the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction by reducing *them* to ashes, having made them an example to those who would live ungodly *lives* thereafter; **7** and *if* He rescued righteous Lot, oppressed by the sensual conduct of unprincipled men **8** (for by what he saw and heard *that* righteous man, while living among them, felt *his* righteous soul tormented day after day by *their* lawless deeds), **9** *then* the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trial, and to keep the unrighteous under punishment for the day of judgment, **10** and especially those who go after the flesh in *its* corrupt lust and despise authority.

The key words of this paragraph is that **the Lord knows how to rescue the godly from trial**, or in other words, He know *whom* to rescue. Even the angels, He **cast [...] into the pit**, as well as the whole **ancient world** and two whole cities. But Noah He preserved as well as Lot, and these are but two examples. Just like in the previous paragraph we should not forget that God will keep His promise to be just, so we must also not forget that He is righteous and will save the godly. This also implies an act of obedience from us to *be godly*!
We also see that God empathizes with man, for He saw Lot's **righteous soul tormented day after day**. God wants men to live godly lives, and for this He needs to set an example, which was in this case to condemn **the cities of Sodom and Gomorrah to destruction**. 
Another comfort for those who seek justice, is that God **[keeps] the unrighteous under punishment for the day of judgment**. Their punishment may not occur in this life, but it will definitely in the next life, which they will spend in hell. And even though they still need to wait until the Day of Judgment, they are **kept under judgment**, just like the **angels who sinned**.

>   Daring, self-willed, they do not tremble when they blaspheme glorious ones, **11** whereas angels who are greater in strength and power do not bring a reviling judgment against them before the Lord.

This is a clear show of arrogance and haughtiness, where the unrighteous **blaspheme glorious** people, something even the **angels who are greater in strength and power do not** do. Angels are beings that are more powerful than humans and have been awarded this to take control of things on Earth, but they know they are accountable for all of their deeds, and therefore they do not dare to **bring a [...] judgment against** the people who have brought much glory to God, for God is pleased with these people (Psalm 147:11). But the unrighteous show their arrogance when **they do not [even] tremble** when blaspheming God's people. 
The next verse explains part of their reason to do so, which is because **they have no knowledge** and "reason" like **animals**.

> **12** But these, like unreasoning animals, born as creatures of instinct to be captured and killed, blaspheming where they have no knowledge, will in the destruction of those creatures also be destroyed,

Animals cannot be blamed for #questions/language/english/spelling ontheiligen, sacrilege? by killing people and spilling blood, but they must still be killed for this (cf. Exodus ... #todo/opzoeken/reference  ). In the same way, these people, who are to be blamed, will **be destroyed**. Though **they have no knowledge** of what they are **blaspheming**, they *do* have knowledge that these **creatures** are *people* whom they are destroying.

> **13** suffering unrighteousness as the wages of *their* unrighteousness, considering it a pleasure to revel in the daytime—*they are* stains and blemishes, reveling in their deceptions, as they feast with you, **14** having eyes full of adultery and unceasing sin, enticing unstable souls, having a heart trained in greed—*they are* accursed children.

Continuing the analogy with animals, the unrighteous are like pigs wallowing in their mud, they cannot be learned not to do so, because it is in thier nature. Another analogy is that they are like rabid dogs, whose only option is to be killed. In the same way the unrighteous are **accursed**. Of course the blood of Jesus is powerful enough to take away any curse, but in this case they do not even want to escape their curse, for they **revel** in the worst of sins. 

> **15** Forsaking the right way, they have gone astray, having followed the way of Balaam, the *son* of Beor, who loved the wages of unrighteousness, **16** but he received a rebuke for his own lawlessness, *for* a mute donkey, speaking out with a voice of a man, restrained the madness of the prophet.

**Balaam** is used here as example because he prophesied out of greed for money. He, however, was saved by his donkey, first because the donkey did not lead him into the hands of the wrathful angel who wanted to kill him, and second because the donkey later explained to him why. Not all of the unrighteous—or better said, no one—will receive this chance. They have been warned already by the examples in Scripture, by the Prophets, as Abraham told the rich man in Jesus' parable (#todo/opzoeken/reference ). 

> **2Pet. 2:17**   These are springs without water and mists driven by a storm, for whom the black darkness has been kept.

The symbolism is stunning. The unrighteous, that is the false prophets and teachers, come up empty. Those who go to them will receive nothing. If there is anything mysterious and invisible, like a mist, then behind it there is **a storm**. Preachers such as #todo/opzoeken/reference (American Gospel, the one with strange eyes) and Benny Hinn, have received millions of dollars over the years and have done "mysterious" miracles, by letting people fall backwards by a simple touch of their hands. Even if that were true and not staged, of what benefit is this to those who need help? It is an empty miracle. 

> **18** For speaking out arrogant *words* of vanity, they entice by sensual lusts of the flesh, those who barely escape from the ones who conducted themselves in error, 

These false teachers **[speak] out arrogant words of vanity**, about being a prophet or being humble. After having received millions of dollars Benny Hinn #tags/theology/falsegospel/wordoffaith #tags/people/christian/fake made a statement that he would never ask for money for his services at the church #todo/opzoeken/reference or for the Gospel. Even if that is true and he never will, he already has accumulated more wealth than he deserved. 
The people who are deceived by these false teachers, are enticed by *their own* **sensual lusts of the flesh**. The false teachers are abominable, however, because the deceived are weak people **who barely escaped** sinful people. 
It is often that people who escaped their own home situation, which would consist of a broken family where it is not unlikely that the parents have divorced and remarried several times, where abuse and addiction were present, will fall for these false teachers because they have a big hunger, or lust, in themselves due to everything they have missed as a child. Young girls from families like these yearn for a father-figure and older men know this and abuse this fact. 

> **19** promising them freedom while they themselves are slaves of corruption; for by what a man is overcome, by this he is enslaved. **20** For if they are overcome, having *both* escaped the defilements of the world by the knowledge of the Lord and Savior Jesus Christ and having again been entangled in them, *then* the last state has become worse for them than the first.

This part explains the fact that these false teachers have not been reborn. A truly reborn Christian would not go back his own vomit. A dog would do so and a sow would go back to the mire, but a dead person ( #todo/opzoeken/reference) who has been reborn as a Christian would not. 
The argument that they have not been reborn is implicit in the statement that these false teachers are **slaves of corruption**. The reborn Christian is slave to nothing other than to Jesus Christ. Peter then explains why they are slaves, which is because they had experienced the **defilements of the world** but had **escaped** these **by the knowledge of the Lord Jesus**. This is like someone who was addicted to drugs but has been freed of it, having experienced true freedom from drugs, or in other words, he has *knowledge* of this beautiful freedom. However, afterwards he is **overcome** by it again and is **again [...] entangled in** it. The only explanation for this is that he was, all the time, still a slave, despite the fact that he experienced freedom.

Another example is that of Onesimus, who also tasted freedom, but was still a slave. However, some might argue that these false teachers were not slaves because they had been **bought** or even *bought free* (v. 1), but this could mean several things. First of all, it could mean that Jesus paid the price for everyone who ever lived on Earth, contrasting the doctrine of Limited Atonement #biblestudy/theology/calvinism/limitedatonement which states that Jesus only died for those who would eventually accept Him as Their Lord and Savior. Jesus paid, but not every person *chose* to be free. So in essence they still are slaves. Second of all, it could mean that these slaves were actually free and chose to be so for a whole, but eventually chose to return to slavery, which indicates that they were free in body, but not in their mind, or in this case with the false teachers, they were free in their minds, but not in their spirit. 
*Once Saved, Always Saved* #biblestudy/theology/calvinism/osas proponents would say then that these have never been "saved", although they mean "reborn", while Arminians would say these have been reborn but have chosen to follow the path that leads to hell. 

> **21** For it would be better for them not to have known the way of righteousness, than having known it, to turn away from the holy commandment handed on to them. **22** The *message* of the true proverb has happened to them, “A DOG RETURNS TO ITS OWN VOMIT,” and, “A sow, after washing, *returns* to wallowing in the mire.”

Many who read this chapter miss out on the important message in vv. 19-20, namely that one is enslaved if he kees going back to that which he has gotten free of. That, actually, is the main message of this chapter—it is to explain the character of the false teachers. These last two verses, and the second part of v. 20, are about the *consequences* of going back to a former "lifestyle" after having been cleansed of it and provides an *additional* example of what these teachers can be compared with. 
This is a general truth of much worth, but is spiritually worth even more. 
In the general case we see so many drug overdoses of famous people, who in most cases had these after they had been clean for a certain period. #tags/people/actors Philip Seymour Hoffman, for example, had stopped using drugs for a while, but when he started using it again, right before his death, he used the same amount as before he quit. However, his body was not used to this amount and so he overdosed. 
We see spiritual examples of this principle when people return to their old ways when they have "experienced"—superficially, might I add—Christianity. When they have returned, they almost always are negative towards Christianity and have to find a good reason to do so in order to defend themselves, or in other words, to be *cognitive dissonant*, which means in simple terms that they have to find a way to be more comfortable with the choices they have made. Examples of critics of Christianity I mentioned earlier are Bart Ehrman and Michael Shermer #tags/people/critics/formerchristians who both claimed to be so-called newborn Christians (although I personally do not think they are) and now have made skepticism towards Christianity their profession.
