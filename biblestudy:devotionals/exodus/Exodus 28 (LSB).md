# Exodus 28 (LSB)

> ### The Priests’ Garments
> **Ex. 28:1**   “Now as for you, bring near to yourself Aaron your brother, and his sons with him, from among the sons of Israel, to minister as priests to Me—Aaron, Nadab and Abihu, Eleazar and Ithamar, Aaron’s sons. **2** “You shall make holy garments for Aaron your brother, for glory and for beauty. **3** “You shall speak to all those wise at heart whom I have ==filled== with the spirit of wisdom, that they make Aaron’s garments to set him apart as holy, in order for him to minister as priest to Me.

The **garments** were meant **to set him apart as holy**. It is not that the clothes made anyone special, but his work required him to be set apart, and also because he was "set apart" in a different sense, he was suited for the work. This seems to be circular reasoning, but let us take the example of a doctor. A doctor is set apart, say, in a special room that is clean, with special clothes, gloves and tools, because his work requires him to be clean. On the other hand, this person was selected as doctor because he had already "set himself apart" by devoting many hours in study and practice to become a doctor. 
Next to that, Aaron's garments also showed the people his authority as a leader. That is why other people were not allowed to wear priestly garments.
