> **Ex. 34:11**   “Be sure to keep what I am commanding you this day: behold, I am going to drive out the Amorite before you, and the Canaanite, the Hittite, the Perizzite, the Hivite, and the Jebusite. 

The colon seems to be placed here at an akward position, but God is in fact **commanding** three things to the Israelites, here and also in vv. 12–13. The first command is to **behold**. However, when God tells someone to *behold* it is because He has something important to show them. Beholding God is what changes people and even when they do not change, it allows them to make their choice. #biblestudy/discovery
