# Mark 8 (LSB)
> ### Jesus Feeds Four Thousand
> **Mark 8:1**   In those days, when there was again a large crowd and they had nothing to eat, Jesus called His disciples and said to them, **1** “I feel compassion for the crowd because they have remained with Me now three days and have nothing to eat. **2** “And if I send them away hungry to their homes, they will faint on the way; and some of them have come from a great distance.” **3** And His disciples answered Him, “Where will anyone be able *to find enough* bread here in *this* desolate place to satisfy these people?” **5** And He was asking them, “How many loaves do you have?” And they said, “Seven.” **6** And He directed the crowd to sit down on the ground; and taking the seven loaves, He gave thanks and broke them. And He kept giving them to His disciples to serve to them, and they served them to the crowd. **7** And they also had a few small fish; and after He blessed them, He ordered these to be served as well. **8** And they ate and were satisfied; and they picked up seven *large* baskets full of what was left over of the broken pieces. **9** Now about four thousand were *there*, and He sent them away. **10** And immediately He entered the boat with His disciples and came to the district of Dalmanutha.
> 
> **Mark 8:11**   And the Pharisees came out and began to argue with Him, seeking from Him a sign from heaven, testing Him. **11** And sighing deeply in His spirit, He said, “Why does this generation seek a sign? Truly I say to you, no sign will be given to this generation.” **12** And leaving them, He again embarked and went away to the other side.
> 
> **Mark 8:14**   And they had forgotten to take bread, and did not have more than one loaf in the boat with them. **13** And He was giving orders to them, saying, “Watch out! Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees and the leaven of Herod.” **14** And they *began to* discuss with one another *the fact* that they had no bread. **15** And Jesus, aware of this, said to them, “Why do you discuss *the fact* that you have no bread? Do you not yet perceive or understand? Do you have a hardened heart? **16** “HAVING EYES, DO YOU NOT SEE? AND HAVING EARS, DO YOU NOT HEAR? And do you not remember, **17** when I broke the five loaves for the five thousand, how many baskets full of broken pieces you picked up?” They said to Him, “Twelve.” **18** “When *I broke* the seven for the four thousand, how many *large* baskets full of broken pieces did you pick up?” And they said to Him, “Seven.” **19** And He was saying to them, “Do you not yet understand?”
> 
> **Mark 8:22**   And they came to Bethsaida. And they brought a blind man to Jesus and pleaded with Him to touch him. **20** And taking the blind man by the hand, He brought him out of the village; and after spitting on his eyes and laying His hands on him, He was asking him, “Do you see anything?” **21** And he looked up and was saying, “I see men, for I see *them* like trees, walking around.”

This indicates that either the man had been able to see before and recognized the trees, or that Jesus healing brought not only sight, but knowledge as well. 

The reason the people looked like trees is because Jesus actually showed the man what these people were. Many seem or claim to be believers, but in fact they are just walking vegetation, alive but not alive at the same time. This is the case with many around us as well and it is for this reason that Jesus warned the man not to enter the village. 
One that has just begun to see clearly should not be among such people. 

>  **22** Then again He laid His hands on his eyes; and he looked intently and was restored, and *began to* see everything clearly. **23** And He sent him to his home, saying, “Do not even enter the village.”

As mentioned in the previous note. Did he *begin* to see clearly or was he already seeing clearly? The same thing can be said of a child, who is pure and clean of sin, but does not see well nor understand what it hears or sees. However, as he grows up he understands the language better, but he also gets to become more acquainted with sin, which ironically limits his understanding.

> ### Peter Confesses Jesus Is the Christ
> **Mark 8:27**   And Jesus went out, along with His disciples, to the villages of Caesarea Philippi; and on the way He was asking His disciples, saying to them, “Who do people say that I am?” **25** And they told Him, saying, “John the Baptist; and others *say* Elijah; but others, one of the prophets.” **26** And He *continued* questioning them, “But who do you say that I am?” Peter answered and said to Him, “You are the Christ.” **27** And He warned them to tell no one about Him.
> 
> **Mark 8:31**   And He began to teach them that the Son of Man must suffer many things and be rejected by the elders and the chief priests and the scribes, and be killed, and after three days rise again. **28** And He was stating the matter openly. And Peter took Him aside and began to rebuke Him. **29** But turning around and seeing His disciples, He rebuked Peter and said, “Get behind Me, Satan! For you are not setting your mind on God’s interests, but man’s.”
> 
> ### Take Up Your Cross
> **Mark 8:34**   And He summoned the crowd with His disciples, and said to them, “If anyone wishes to come after Me, he must deny himself, and take up his cross and follow Me. **30** “For whoever wishes to save his life will lose it, but whoever loses his life for My sake and the gospel’s will save it. **31** “For what does it profit a man to gain the whole world, and forfeit his soul? **32** “For what will a man give in exchange for his soul? **33** “For whoever is ashamed of Me and My words in this adulterous and sinful generation, the Son of Man will also be ashamed of him when He comes in the glory of His Father with the holy angels.”
