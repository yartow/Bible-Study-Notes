All Scripture is God-breathed (2 Timothy 3:16-17)
And in 1 Corinthians 10:6ff it says that all these things occurred to them as examples to us ... etc. see v.12 do not fall. 
So the Holy Spirit inspired Moses to write these books for us as end-time congregations to be warned. 

And so it says:

> Exodus 15:22-27 (ESV) Bitter Water Made Sweet
> 22 Then Moses made Israel set out from the Red Sea, and they went into the wilderness of Shur. They went three days in the wilderness and found no water. 23 When they came to Marah ==which means bitter in Hebrew==, they could not drink the water of Marah because it was bitter; therefore it was named Marah. 24 And the people grumbled against Moses, saying, “What shall we drink?” 25 And he cried to the LORD, and the LORD showed him a log, and he threw it into the water, and the water became sweet.
> There the LORD made for them a statute and a rule, and there he tested them, 26 saying, “If you will diligently listen to the voice of the LORD your God, and do that which is right in his eyes, and give ear to his commandments and keep all his statutes, I will put none of the diseases on you that I put on the Egyptians, for I am the LORD, your healer.”
> 27 Then they came to Elim, where there were twelve springs of water and seventy palm trees, and they encamped there by the water.

We moeten 
understand that this is not an Exodus. They had just sung a song and went from Egypt. But they arrived in the desert where there was no water, but only heat. No view, but only pain. But finally they see water, but they couldn't drink of this. 
Lord, what is this? Is this a joke? The water is bitter and we're thirsty. 
But what happened then? We've just read it. I believe the Holy Spirit has written this specifically for us. The Lord called to Moses and showed him a piece of wood. 
This is the message that I have on my ever since the things happened last week. The wood is the cross. And everything  that happens, all the bitterness and pain, the water can only become sweet through the cross. 
As a congregation months ago we've prayed: form us in the likeness of your son. This is it, it will not become different. the only way for us not to become embittered, is to drink it through the cross. 
The prophets had to eat the word, and it was bitter at first, but then later it became sweet. 
Not because we or they like pain, but because all things work for the good. What is "good"? It's not the things that we like, but "good = being equal to Christ". 
I've prayed abou tthis. What does it mean to be saved by faith. Do we have to work? 

In 1 Corinthians 10:6 we've read 
> 1 Corinthians 10:6 (ESV) 6 Now these things took place as examples for us, that we might not desire evil as they did.

At pesach we saw blood at the doorposts. In the desert, where they are right now. They are still in the world, and the Lord wants to use the desert to form us so that as many of us as possible will go to the Promised Land. That's why we can only praise and thank God as congregation for the difficulties we have every day—Paul says that we boast in our suppressions, it's not good for the flesh, but it'll eventually lead to hope. For the hope does not shame us, cf.  Romans 5. 
And so we will bear the image of heaven. And the Father will raise us as well. That is the foresight we have towards the Promised Land. 
In the same way the Lord Jesus learned obedienced, even though He was the Son, the *learned* obedience. How did He learn? By suffering. But when he learned, he had become the salvation Himself for us. 
If we are saved, then nothing will be left us. nothing at all anymore. 

God has means—which some call means of grace: prayer, coming together, encouraging each other. We can put these things aside, stop prayer, etc. Some people will do that. But there is one thing we cannot escape: the work of the Holy Spirit. 
The bitter water, we will never be able to escape, because God has said that whoever follows Him will be persecuted. And especially in a local congregation, we will sharpen each other (cf. Proverbs 27:17). There are brothers here who have complaints about each other. But we should not be like this. 
Know that every person that irritates you is to mold you and sharpen you. This brother is there to make you grow. And when you respond from the flesh, it brings forth nothing except for death. 
You can either kill the flesh through the spirit or the other way around. What you need to do is: when someone accuses you, you need to not accuse the other back. 
If these situations have occurred, we need to pray that we will be formed in his likeness. If we have cancer and we pray that God takes it away, we shouldn't be surprised when God brings a scalpel and starts cutting. It will hurt. 

The Israelites complained, where's the food? where's the water? 
And when they saw the Mannah, they said: "What's that?" for that is what the woord "manna" means. 
Everything that happens in these last few months, is so much better than what we could have prayed and asked for from God. 
This is the real manna from heaven. 
We bless it, we talk about it, but in practice, we just want to turn our back and leave all of this. 

On the top of the mountain it's nice to be together, but it's in the depths of the valley where God really is with us. 
Let us realize this and full of convicted say: God, you are before me and knead and mold me, even though it hurts. 

You need to realize, they were with 300.000 men, and there are 12 springs. That surely wouldn't have been enough. They would've needed to line up, and share the water. This is what we need to learn as well. We need to learn how to get the water. This is my prayer. Amen.
