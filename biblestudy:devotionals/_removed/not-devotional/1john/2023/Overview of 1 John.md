# Overview of 1 John

## An overview of 1 John 1

* What we have seen, we want to proclaim, because this will make us joyful (vv.1-4). 
* This is the message we proclaim: God is light (v.5)
  * Only when we walk in this light, we are Christians and will receive his forgiveness (vv.7,9)
  * If we walk in the dark or say we do not sin, we are liars and we do not have his forgiveness. (vv.6,8,10).

## An overview of 1 John 2

* By the way, I am writing this to you so that you will not sin (v.1)
  * but if you should sin, know then that we have an advocate Whom we know, Jesus Christ. (vv.1-2)
    * How do we know we know Jesus? If we keep His commandments. (v.3)
      * You can only say you know Him if you act on it as well. (vv.4-6)
    * This commandment is not a new one but we do look differently at it after the coming of Christ. (vv.7-8)
      * This commandment is only understood by those who love their brothers. (v.10)
      * Those who claim to be in the Light, but hate their brothers, were never Christians. (vv.9,11)
* ==These are the reasons why I have written to you (the above) and am still writing to you (the next part), and I address three groups in my letter: little children, fathers and young men. (vv.12-14)==
* Do not love the world, for if you do, you are not of the Father. (vv.15-17)
* This world is passing away and we are nearing the end-times. (v.17 until 3:2 or v.3 with new pericope starting from v.4)
  * There are many who seem to be from us, but in fact are not (vv.18-19)
    * But you already know the truth because of your anointing with the Holy Spirit (vv.20-21)
      * and you also know who are those pretending to be of Christ (the antichrists) (vv.22-23)
      * that's why you should be different and abide by the commandment ==so that== you will have eternal life (vv.24-25)
  * The things above are about those who try to deceive you, but now I am going to write about you, even though you do not need anyone to teach you (v.26-27)
    * (I do want to tell you this though) abide by the commandment, that you have confidence before Jesus when manifests Himself (appears again) (vv.27-28)
    * How can you have confidence? Everyone *like* Him will be *of* Him, and He is righteous, so *be* righteous. (v.29)
      * There is even more confidence to obtain through this: the world does not know us (which is because it does not know God, so this means we are of God) (3:1)
      * Once again, we *are* children of God and we will be like Him once He is manifested (3:2) (see explanation on what it means to be "like Him")
      * For this reason we purify ourselves right now already. (see example of princess who prepares herself)
