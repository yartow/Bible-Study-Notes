God gave me love for people and made me a fisher of man.

Why streams of blessings in the Bible, but only drops in mine and in those around me. 
Half a year later I started studying theology (while I used to study Economy before that). 

I didn't know much about other cultures. 
"Would you come and help me with my refugee ministry? someone asked me.

Sri Lanka. 
But people in my church started explaining about the noise of the translation in the background. 

The Dutch didn't know how to tell about Jesus or to disciple others (of different ethnicity). 

I never pastored and planted a church and most certainly not an intercultural church. 
We made every possible mistake. But helped us, because we have Him in our hearts. 
In that context we were the first who ever planted churches. 

I really didn't know how to disciple them. 
So again I was frustrated. 
Hard and resistant secular world on the side.

I studied about revivals and I asked God to do that in the Netherlands. 
Isaiah 43. Forget about the former things. I will do a new thing. 

People in the Netherlands asked me to do what I did in Rotterdam. 

I saw the advantage of a big church but also of smaller groups. Dilemma. But God said: you don't have to choose, you can have both. 

Then I got to know DMM and I realized my mistake. 
I needed to equip them from scratch. 
Starting by writing the names of our beloved and pray for them.
