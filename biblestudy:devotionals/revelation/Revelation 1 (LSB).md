> THE REVELATION OF JESUS CHRIST
> 
> **Rev. 1:1**   The Revelation of Jesus Christ, which God gave Him to show to His slaves the things which must soon happen; and He indicated *this* by sending *it* through His angel to His slave John, **2** who bore witness to the word of God and to the witness of Jesus Christ, *even* to all that he saw. **3** Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy and keep the things which are written in it, for the time is near.

This book is a *single* **Revelation**, not multiple revelations. There are multiple events that happen over multiple periods, but it is one story. In fact, the whole Bible, though its story is spread out over millenia and across three continents, is just one story. This book is merely one chapter of this story. If the Earth and the universe have truly existed for about 6,600 years (according to Young Earth Creationists, which is what I believe as well) #biblestudy/apologetics/creation/young, and if we interpret Revelation correctly, there are an unknown number of years until the Great Tribulation comes, which will last for seven years, after which there will be another period of 1,000 years. So this book would only cover a seventh of all of history, a chapter one could say, revealing only one thing, hence the singular form, namely the end of history on Earth as we know it, the end of mankind as he is right now in his sinful nature and the end of the Satan and all his servantsl demons.

Blessed is he who reads and those who hear the words of the prophecy and keep the things which are written in it, for the time is near.”

The reason why **he who reads** is singular while the next phrase is plural, is because it is assumed one person is reading this to the congregation. Note that this blessing only comes when one **[keeps] the things [...] written** in this book. However, what commandments are there in Revelation? It is a book that describes prophecies concerning the future. #biblestudy/questions/revelation Not a book that gives new or repeats old commandments. However, it does mention examples warning us to stay in His Word (through examples of churches (congregations) that did not (almost all of the seven churches #todo/opzoeken), to persevere, to listen to His voice and act on it (Revelation 3:20, but see notes there), and not to follow the Beast, Antichrist or the riches of this world. 


> MESSAGE TO THE SEVEN CHURCHES
> 
> **Rev. 1:4**   John to the seven churches that are in Asia: Grace to you and peace, from the One who is and who was and who is to come, and from the seven Spirits who are before His throne, **5** and from Jesus Christ, the faithful witness, the firstborn of the dead, and the ruler of the kings of the earth. 

This message is from *three Persons*, an unknown **One**, the **seven Spirits** and **Jesus Christ**. This indicates the work of the Trinity, although critics might point to the problem of the *seven Spirits* which is not the same as the *Holy Spirit*.  ,

> To Him who loves us and released us from our sins by His blood— **6** and He has made us *to be* a kingdom, priests to His God and Father—to Him *be* the glory and the might forever and ever. Amen. **7** BEHOLD, HE IS COMING WITH THE CLOUDS, and EVERY EYE WILL SEE HIM, EVEN THOSE WHO PIERCED HIM; and all the tribes of the earth will MOURN OVER HIM. Yes, amen.

This is not to say that everyone will mourn for Jesus, even those who pierced Him. By far not. Most people will mourn because they made the wrong decision and realize they are too late now. I do not know if this is what John meant with this verse, but in every tribe there will be people who mourn over Jesus like we mourn over His death during Good Friday #tags/events/goodfriday and remember His suffering, for by the time Jesus returns, the Gospel shall have spread to every tribe (cf. > **Rev. 1:8**   “I am the Alpha and the Omega,” says the Lord God, “who is and who was and who is to come, the Almighty.”

The unknown Person **who is and who was and who is to come** mentioned in v. 4 is now revealed, it is **the Lord God, [...] the Almighty**.


> THE VISION OF THE SON OF MAN
> 
> **Rev. 1:9**   I, John, your brother and fellow partaker in the tribulation and kingdom and perseverance *which are* in Jesus, was on the island called Patmos because of the word of God and the witness of Jesus. **10** I was in the Spirit on the Lord’s day, and I heard behind me a loud voice like a trumpet, **11** saying, “Write in a scroll what you see, and send *it* to the seven churches: to Ephesus and to Smyrna and to Pergamum and to Thyatira and to Sardis and to Philadelphia and to Laodicea.”
> 
> **Rev. 1:12**   Then I turned to see the voice that was speaking with me. And having turned I saw seven golden lampstands; **13** and in the middle of the lampstands *I saw* one like a son of man, clothed in a robe reaching to the feet, and girded across His chest with a golden sash. **14** And His head and His hair were white like white wool, like snow; and His eyes were like a flame of fire. **15** His feet *were* like burnished bronze, when it has been made to glow in a furnace, and His voice *was* like the sound of many waters, **16** and having in His right hand seven stars, and a sharp two-edged sword which comes out of His mouth, and His face was like the sun shining in its power.
> 
> **Rev. 1:17**   And when I saw Him, I fell at His feet like a dead man. And He placed His right hand on me, saying, “Do not fear; I am the first and the last, **18** and the living One; and I was dead, and behold, I am alive forever and ever, and I have the keys of death and of Hades. **19** Therefore write the things which you have seen, and the things which are, and the things which will take place after these things. **20** As for the mystery of the seven stars which you saw in My right hand, and the seven golden lampstands: the seven stars are the angels of the seven churches, and the seven lampstands are the seven churches.
