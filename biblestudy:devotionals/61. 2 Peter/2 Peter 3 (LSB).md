# 2 Peter 3 (LSB)
### The Day of the Lord Will Come
> **2Pet. 3:1**   This is now, beloved, the second letter I am writing to you in which I am stirring up your sincere mind by way of reminder, **2** that you should remember the words spoken beforehand by the holy prophets and the commandment of the Lord and Savior *spoken* by your apostles,

The theme of this letter is described here. It is to *remind* the readers of **mockers**, who will incite them to follow **after their own lusts** and who deny the Second Coming of Jesus Christ. These mockers follow after their own lusts and therefore seduce their audience to do the same. Christian must **remember** the Old Testament and the New Testament, for *both* prophesy Jesus' return and His victory over this world. The importance of this cannot be stressed enough. Even though there many books in the Bible and even though 2 Peter is one of the shortest letters, the hope that Jesus *will come back* is what has kept Christians going on in these last two millennia. There might even be more millennia to come (although the signs tell that His coming is soon, and we should live like that), but Christ *will* come, and He will deal with all the false teachers. 

> **3** knowing this first of all, that in the last days mockers will come with *their* mocking, following after their own lusts, **4** and saying, “Where is the promise of His coming? For since the fathers fell asleep, all continues just as it was from the beginning of creation.”

Their **lusts** might not seem to be related to this mocking, but it is the very reason they are doing so. It is only because they deny Jesus' coming that they can appease their own minds while they are sinning. For if Jesus does not come back, they have nothing to fear. Now they *know* that this is not true, but they lie to themselves, not because they have logically come to this conclusion, but they *want* to sin and then make all kinds of excuses to be able to do so. 
**Example.** In this day it is not that women have done research and come to the conclusion that it is economically and ethically better that women have the right to an abortion, but rather it is the other way around. They want to have free sex without bearing the consequences thereof and *therefore* they approve of abortion. #tags/politics/abortion

> **5** For when they maintain this, it escapes their notice that by the word of God *the* heavens existed long ago and *the* earth was formed out of water and by water, 

These mockers or scoffers *deliberately* overlook the fact that God created everything. (The NIV uses this word.) In this day people could be slightly excused for believing that this world has come into being by evolution, because it is taught in school and believed by almost everyone around us, but in Peter's time everyone knew that it was either the Lord Yahweh Who created the world, or another god whom they believed in. Most likely, it is the former, because Peter is speaking of noticing that God created the world through His words (v. 7). This would also mean then that the false teachers are those who know about this and are either pagans who heard of this Gospel or Jews who try to lead the Christians astray.

> **6** through which the world at that time was destroyed, being deluged with water. **7** But by His word the present heavens and earth are being reserved for fire, being kept for the day of judgment and destruction of ungodly men.

Those things with which God created He will also destroy. He used the Word to create water and out of water the Earth. Afterwards with water He destroyed everything which was *on* Earth. Later God will use His Word to destroy Earth itself *and* the heavens, which are probably the whole atmosphere and all of the heavenly bodies. Perhaps even outer space itself is destroyed.

> **2Pet. 3:8**   But do not let this one *fact* escape your notice, beloved, that with the Lord one day is like a thousand years, and a thousand years like one day.

This has nothing to do with a translation of time periods, such as that the creation days are actually six periods of billions of years each. This would not make sense, because each evolutionary #questions/language/english/spelling period has a different duration and the order according to natural evolution is different from that of creation as well. 
Rather, Peter is only saying this because Jesus said the end-times is "near" and that He (and others) say that He is coming "soon" and people did not see Him (v.4). Terms like "soon" and "slow" are explained further in v.9, but have to be seen in context. 

> **9** The Lord is not slow about His promise, as some consider slowness, but is patient toward you, not willing for any to perish but for all to come to repentance.

**The Lord is [...] patient toward *you***. He is also patient toward the rest of the world, because He wants them to be saved before He returns, but why would He be patient toward "you"—by whom are meant the recipients of this letter, who are *Christians*? The Lord has patience even towards the Christians, though not because of any possibility that they might perish, but because there were some in the group of recipients of this letter who were *not Christians*. Even though the address is to the diaspora (1 Peter 1:1) to those who *believe* (2 Peter 1:1) there could still be some who did not completely believe or fully live the life of a Christian. See also [[1 John 1 (LSB)/Essay. The homogeneity of recipients of Biblical epistles.]]

Note that the word “**willing**” does not refer to *God’s will*, which will always happen, but it is His *desire*. God’s desire is that everyone chooses the right path and become more like Jesus, but in His will He allows people to make their own choices. Despite the fact that eight billion people all do what they want themselves, what happens on Earth is still according to what God *wills*. The fact there are wars going on between Ukraine and Russia, and between Israel and Palestine #tags/history/war/israel/2023#, are the consequence of people’s choice but they only occur because God *willed* them. If God did not will them, despite people’s free choice,  there would have been no war. 

> **10** But the day of the Lord will come like a thief, in which the heavens will pass away with a roar and the elements will be destroyed with intense heat, and the earth and its works will be found *out*.

Perhaps another reason for being so patient is to let the unfaithful doze in and not expect the **day of the Lord** anymore and let it **come like a thief** in the night (cf. 2 Peter 3:4). On the day itself, however, there will be no one who will not notice. 
This will be the day that the Lord Jesus Christ returns to Earth and the last day for Earth to exist (and perhaps outer space as well, including all the planets, see notes on v. 7). A not very  frequent question is how it is possible that the whole world will be able to see Him *at the same time*. Some answer that this has now been made possible through the Internet. In my view, it is not as simple as that, for not everyone has Internet and not everyone would be awake at the time when Jesus comes back. But if **the heavens will pass away with a roar** and many other things will happen that will definitely wake people up out of their sleep, then no will be asleep anymore. Aside from that, Jesus will show Himself throughout the world *simultaneously* in a miraculous way. There is no other way to explain that but through miracles. Even a solar eclipse is seen a few minutes later in different countries and as soon as Jesus lands, no one would be able to see Him anymore. The answer is that we simply do not know how we will be able to see Him, and even though He said He would return on the Mount of Olives, we also do not know how He can be in one place only and still be seen by the world. Even if there were people recording this and putting it on the Internet, I doubt whether any system still works after **the elements [have been] destroyed with intense heat**. 

> **2Pet. 3:11**   Since all these things are to be destroyed in this way, what sort of people ought you to be in holy conduct and godliness, 

I am still wondering why the authors of the LSB did not add a question mark here, even though this is clearly a question, even if a rhetorical one. #biblestudy/questions/2peter

> **12** looking for and hastening the coming of the day of God, because of which the heavens burning will be destroyed, and the elements will melt with intense heat! 

It is interesting that Peter uses the word **hastening**, most likely implying that the **day of God** can change. If that is what he means, it is something like how Abraham “changed” God’s mind in saving Lot from Sodom before He destroyed the city, or how Moses “changed” God’s mind in destroying the people of Israel. Both men did not actually change God’s plans. Some say that God still destroyed Israel, but delayed it because of Moses’ pleas. However, God can see the future and has also seen the roles of those who plea. My understanding of the above examples is that God said He would destroy Sodom and wait for Abraham to intervene and respectfully ask if He would save Lot and his family #todo/opzoeken/reference . In the same way God did not *change* the timing of the destruction of Israel when Moses intervened #todo/opzoeken/reference , but He already had in mind to destroy them at the times when this actually happened (e.g. during the invasion of the Assyrians, Babylonians and Persians #todo/opzoeken/reference ). In human terms, one can say that God *threatened* to destroy Israel or that He was *testing* Moses to see if he would intervene, but these words are limited to our understanding of the meaning of those. In English there is a term called *4D chess*, which means that a person’s plans are so intricate and well-thought, foreseeing all kinds of possible scenarios. The phrase that *God was playing 4D chess with Moses* would better describe His actions. The Bible and Christians use the description from Isaiah, namely that *His ways are higher than ours* (Isaiah 55:8-9). 

> **13** But according to His promise we are looking for NEW HEAVENS AND A NEW EARTH, in which righteousness dwells.

"Heavens" is plural here. What would that mean? According to Kent Hovind #tags/people/christian there are three uses of this word. The first refers to the sky and the atmosphere and even outer space, for example when Gods Abraham to look at the stars “in heaven” #todo/opzoeken/reference . The second use of this word refers to God’s residence. But Hovind says there is a third heaven, which is something that is even “higher” than outer space, and like a layer surrounding outer space. This, of course, can neither be proven nor disproven. 
God is in heaven, but will live on Earth. However, will the New Earth still be a planet, that is a sphere "hanging" in space? #todo/opzoeken/reference Job 
Does "heaven" here refer to God's residing place because He will dwell with man, or to the atmosphere or outer space? #biblestudy/questions/2peter 

> ### Be on Your Guard
> **2Pet. 3:14**   Therefore, beloved, since you are looking for these things, be diligent to be found by Him in peace, spotless and blameless, 

If we are **looking for** the Day of the Lord (v. 12), that is looking for Him to come and see us, then we should also **be diligent to be found by Him** in all the ways that He desires. 
**Example.** People, and especially children, who are busy doing the things they should not do, are hoping their parents will not come back and find them at the very time that they are doing these things. Rather yet, perhaps a bit hypocritical, they hope that their parents will see them at the right moment when they are doing the good things. Christians, however, should hope that Jesus returns as quickly as possible and therefore will live in His way *all the time*.

> **15** and consider the patience of our Lord *as* salvation, 

There is a statement within this sentence, which is "**the patience of our Lord [should be considered] as salvation**". Christians should accept this as the truth. What does this mean, then? A similar phrase, such as when someone receives a punishment and where the punisher says, "Consider this mercy", would mean that actually there would have been a much harsher punishment. 
In this case the Christians Peter is speaking to are waiting for Jesus' return and are diligently working (v. 14) and should not consider His "delay" as idleness or forgetfulness of God, but as **salvation**. Does this mean then that these Christians can consider themselves as those who will be saved, just like in the situation where a person applies for a job and is waiting for a response, and where the employer says, "If you do not hear anything from us, consider this as a rejection"? 
To put this a little bit more in context, the Christians here are looking for the coming of the day of God (v. 12) where heavens will burn and elements will melt. However, they are not looking for the destruction of the world, but they are looking for **new heavens and a new Earth** (v. 13). For *those* people *only* the patience of the Lord can be considered as salvation. Paraphrased this sentence could be written as follows. #composed/bible/2peter
> Therefore, since you are looking for these things, you may consider the patience of the Lord as salvation.
What a wonderful promise this is for those who are seeking His return! #biblestudy/discovery 

> just as also our beloved brother Paul, according to the wisdom given him, wrote to you, **16** as also in all *his* letters, speaking in them of these things, in which are some things hard to understand, 

Even Peter admits here that either Paul’s writing is **hard to understand** or that the matter he writes about is difficult, and indeed, there has been much debate about the controversial doctrines put forward in Paul’s epistles such as head coverings, and the role of women on the pulpit and in the board of elders and deacons.

> which the untaught and unstable distort, as *they do* also the rest of the Scriptures, to their own destruction. 

There are many people with academic degrees who still **distort** Paul's writings, but they are **untaught** by the Holy Spirit, for what they have been taught is by themselves and not by the Bible. Every distortion of Christianity, such Jehova's Witness #tags/religion/jw and Mormonism #tags/religion/lds, are made up by people who thought up different interpretations of the Bible because they could not live with the truth of the Bible. The Bible speaks of a Holy Trinity[^1] but also of there being only one God, and both sects have problems with this doctrine. Every other distortion such as the allowance for pastors to women or gay #tags/sex/lgbt all come from a person or a group of persons who taught themselves on how to differently interpret the Bible because they could live with its views. 

Aside from the meaning of this part, this verse also implies the epistles of Paul to be  canonical, because of the phrase "**the rest of the Scriptures**". So in other words, Paul's letters are compared to Scripture. This is not a very strong argument, in my view, of the testimony of the canonicity of Paul's Epistles. 

> **17** You therefore, beloved, knowing this beforehand, be on your guard lest you, having been carried away by the error of unprincipled men, fall from your own steadfastness,

**[U]nprincipled men** are those who have no principles. The so-called teachers and prophets lead people astray because of their own lusts, and it does not bother them that they lead people straight into hell. These are the worst kind of people, those who have no principles. Even anti-heroes in movies #tags/movies such as *Venom* have principles and do not hurt the innocent, which is why in the view of the audience they are still somewhat of a hero, but these teachers and prophets have no restrictions on whom they mislead.
Of course, on the outside when looking at people like Joel Osteen and Joseph Prince, Kenneth Copeland #tags/theology/falsegospel/prosperity and honorable mentions to Tom de Wal and Jermell Witherspoon[^2]  #tags/people/christian/fake, they seem to be nice people and seem much less bad than for example gangsters, but gangsters do not know what they are doing (cf. 1 John 2:11), but teachers of the false gospel have studied the Bible and they deliberately choose to teach some doctrines, leave other out and falsify others, *knowingly* leading people away from the truth and straight into hell.

> **18** but grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. To Him *be* the glory, both now and to the day of eternity. Amen.

One interpretation of growing in grace is simply that Christians should grow *while* they are in the grace of Christ, but this interpretation fails because of the second part, namely that the Christian needs to grow in the **knowledge of [...] Jesus Christ**. In other words, he needs to learn more about Jesus and know Him more as a person, or simply put, gain more knowledge. So this means that the Christian should also grow ***in* the grace** or gain more grace. It is the same interpretation of when people say "grow in knowledge, grow in size, grow in height". 
How does one **grow in the grace**? It does not mean that the grace is increased, because the grace **of our Lord** that is meant here, is the gift of our **Savior Jesus Christ**. I will say it once again, Jesus Christ *is* the gift, He *is* the grace the Father has given us. The Father did not send His Son twice or given *more* than His Son. Of course, there is rain and sunshine and these are part of His grace as well[^3], but again, that is not the type of grace meant here. So if we consider only Jesus Christ as grace, the growth can be interpreted as *accepting more* of His grace. Christians who accepted Jesus as their Savior still have a daily struggle to give all glory to Christ for *every single thing* in their lives. It is so easy to give credit to ourselves for something *we* (think we) have done, and it is so hard to realize that everything we have been able to do, is only by the grace of God. Whether this is practical or spiritual, it is all by the grace of God. Take for example the case of "converting" another person. One might have studied and prepared for what he is going to say to an unbeliever, he has spent time and effort on going out and reach out to people, but it is still God Who sent this person on his path and prepared and opened his heart at the right time.
