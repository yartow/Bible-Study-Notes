> “Jude, a slave of Jesus Christ, and brother of James, To those who are the called, beloved in God the Father, and kept for Jesus Christ: May mercy and peace and love be multiplied to you. 

Do you see that Jude used the word **kept** here. What does this mean other than that **God the Father** has kept us all these years that we have been alive. Are you alive? What is the reason that you are alive? You might think that this is because you are breathing, and eating and sleeping, because these are the only three things you need to stay alive. But we—as Christians—know that "to live" in the *human dictionary* is not the same as "to live" in the *Christian dictionary*. As a matter of fact Jesus even uses a different word to distinguish between "to breathe", like so many of those out in the world are doing right now, and to actually *live* like Jesus meant for us to live. 
We all know the words of Jesus Christ which He said in John 10:10. Maybe some of you have even memorized those: 

> The thief comes only to steal and kill and destroy; I came that they may have life, and have *it* abundantly.

This abundant life—and there is even a book #tags/books/christian named after this, eponymously *The Abundant Life* by Ray Baughman—is the life that Jesus Christ is speaking of here. It is, in even better words, also called *eternal life*, as John speaks of in among others 1 John 1:2. 
But much more than just living, Jude here is speaking of the verb "to keep". Now what do you do when you want to keep something? 
**Example.** I, for example, really like chocolate, and I also really like cookies. Now what I have at home, is even better than, namely it is chocolate cookies. Fortunately, I have a wife who does not like chocolate cookies, she has her own snacks, which I do not particularly enjoy, such sour plums and seasoned sunflower seeds. So this works, if it were not for the fact that my wife is so generous and hospitable, that whenever guests come to our house, she gives them what they like. And to my unfortunate benefit, our guests do not like sour plums or seasoned sunflowers seeds, but they prefer the unhealthy snacks such as chocolate, cookies or even worse, chocolate cookies. And so—for this reason—I have a place in my cupboard, in one of my cupboards—which I will not tell you, of course—where I keep my secret stash. And whenever I need something, I put it there, where I *keep* it from unwanted people who are after my stash. 

Now, in the same way, there is someone who is after God's favorite stash, which is His children, which is us, Christians. The devil is after His children and wants to do nothing more than take us away from Him, or, if he cannot, he will go after those who are not His children *yet*, for those who *are* His children cannot be taken from God's hands (John 10:28)—although they can be distracted from doing good.
