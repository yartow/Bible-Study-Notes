David Pawson #tags/people/christian in his *Unlocking the Bible* seminars compared the personality of Ezra with that of Nehemiah. Ezra is the one who mourned in himself when encountering disaster such as in Ezra 9:3 and pulls out *some* of the hairs of *his own* head and beard, while Nehemiah rages in anger and pulls out the hairs (it does not say some) of *others*. Although the two definitely have different personalities, the contrast, however, is not as big as it seems. See also notes on [[Nehemiah 4 (LSB)|Nehemiah 4:4]].
