Goldsmiths work in very precise way as every gram of gold is worth a lot of money. In melting and mixing gold the are constantly aware of the temperature, so that no gold will be lost[^1]. If they also make jewelry they work with precision tools. In short, they would not seem like construction builders who build walls. Still, the goldsmiths made these crude repairs. No work was below them, regardless of their education and skills.

[^1]: In fact, gold will not evaporate that easily. It has an ideal evaporation temperature around 1,400°C and a boiling point of 2,856°C. Other metals in the gold, however, evaporate at lower temperatures and refine the gold. However, if the gold has been refined, but is sold as unrefined gold, say 22 carats or less, which costs less, the goldsmith will lose money on this.
