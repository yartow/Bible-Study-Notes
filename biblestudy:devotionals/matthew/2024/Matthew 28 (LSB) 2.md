> **Matt. 28:8**   And they left the tomb quickly with fear and great joy and ran to report it to His disciples.

#### Essay. The real meaning of fearing God
How is it that someone can leave **with fear and great joy** at the same time? The fear mentioned here does not refer to v. 5, and even if it did, their fear would have been gone, because the women believed they were angels to guide them to where Jesus was, not to punish them. This did not apply to the guards though, who might have still been afraid up to this point. 
However, it is too simple to just say—like some do—that fearing God simply means respecting Him. For true respect there is another word, which is actually only meant for God—*awe*. *Fear* goes beyond respect. The fear the women felt here is the same feeling any messenger has when he has an important message to bring to, say, the troops, which could change the outcome of a war. It is not any excitement caused by the possibility of losing this message and letting it fall into the wrong hands, but it is the realization that it is a great mission, and that it needs to be done with utmost care. It is like the attitude of a slave who wants to do his best for his master, not because he is afraid of him—even though he does fear him—but simply because he understands his responsibility. 
The difference between "fear" and "being afraid" here, in the example of the slave, is that the slave constantly lives in "fear" and realizes his inferior position to his master, but has nothing to be afraid of as long as he does his work well. It is only when he disobeys his master, that he becomes afraid. In the same way Christians ought to fear God—all the time—but do not have be afraid all the time, when they abide in Him.

> ### THE GREAT COMMISSION
> 
> **Matt. 28:16**   But the eleven disciples proceeded to Galilee, to the mountain which Jesus had designated. **17** And when they saw Him, they worshiped *Him*; but some doubted. **18** And Jesus came up and spoke to them, saying, “All authority has been given to Me in heaven and on earth. **19** Go therefore and make disciples of all the nations, baptizing them in the name of the Father and the Son and the Holy Spirit, **20** teaching them to keep all that I commanded you; and behold, I am with you always, even to the end of the age.”
