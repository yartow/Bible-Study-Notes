> ### Signs of Christ’s Return
> **Matt. 24:1**   And coming out from the temple, Jesus was going along, and His disciples came up to point out the temple buildings to Him. **2** And He answered and said to them, “Do you not see all these things? Truly I say to you, not one stone here will be left upon another, which will not be torn down.”

What did Jesus mean by this? It was the disciples who pointed these buildings to Him, so of course they had seen it. However, as usual, Jesus was pointing to the metaphysical things. The buildings everyone could see, but they could not see that these buildings would not be there anymore in a few decades. Jesus could even see the details, namely that **not one stone [would] be left upon another**, which was a literal reference to t the destruction of the Temple by fire. The gold in the inside of the Temple (see notes on Leviticus on the construction of the Temple and the gold plated wood) had apparently dripped onto the stones and the Romans had turned every stone in order to get as much gold as they could from the ruins. 
A spiritual lesson that can be learned here—although this was not Jesus' point of His message, which is to follow later—is that Jesus sees much more than the human eye. He can see even in the dimension of time, but more (important) than that, He can also see in the spiritual dimension. Those buildings that look magnificent and beautiful will be nothing more than a pile of rubble of which all gold is stripped. Likewise, men should not be impressed with the splendor and glory of this world—it is vulnerable to time. Every mighty man becomes old and every beautiful woman gets wrinkles some day. The glory of this world fades away (#todo/opzoeken/reference 1 Corinthians 5:17) but His Word remains forever. 

> **Matt. 24:3**   Now as He was sitting on the Mount of Olives, the disciples came to Him privately, saying, “Tell us, when will these things happen, and what *will be* the sign of Your coming and of the end of the age?”
> **Matt. 24:4**   And Jesus answered and said to them, “See to it that no one deceives you. **5** “For many will come in My name, saying, ‘I am the Christ,’ and will deceive many. 

Jesus does not directly answer the Apostles' questions, but starts off with a warning about deceivers who will claim to be **the Christ**. This is to prevent that if they should still have questions about the end-times #tags/theology/eschatology after this, they should not believe anyone else but Jesus Christ Himself. This is first of all a warning, but second of all—though not mentioned here but in other parts of the New Testament—it is also in itself a sign of the end-times, for there will be many deceivers who claim to be the Christ #todo/opzoeken/reference. 

> **6** “And you are going to hear of wars and rumors of wars. See that you are not alarmed, for *those things* must take place, but *that* is not yet the end. **7** “For nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom, and in various places there will be famines and earthquakes. **8** “But all these things are *merely* the beginning of birth pains.
‭‭
Many Christians have forgotten about this warning of Jesus, namely that we should not be alarmed. Every major event in the last couple of years, or even decades, are in the first place only precursors to **those things [that] must take place**, which Jesus referred to, and even these things are **not yet the end**. As Jesus said, **these things are merely the beginning of birth pains**, after that it will still take an indefinite amount of time before the proverbial baby is born. 
So everything we see happening right now, will only get worse. This is one thing that man is not able—or perhaps not *willing*—to understand. As God's ways are higher than ours, so we cannot understand what is to come, for what will come has never happened before on Earth. 
The events mentioned here are wars, **famines and earthquakes** but of much greater proportions than man has ever seen, far greater than that of the World Wars where despite famine most people survived. Even in China and Russia, where supposedly[^1] 150 and 100 million people died, respectively. Also, where we have seen in the World Wars mostly one country, such as Germany and Japan, rising against others—and in the history of Israel multiple countries fighting against e.g. Assyria—in the end-times we can expect nations to fight against each other. 
As for earthquakes, the biggest one had a hundred thousand casualties. This will be minimal compared to the earthquakes in the end-time, which might easily surpass millions of casualties. 
‭‭ 
> **Matt. 24:9**   “Then they will deliver you to tribulation, and will kill you, and you will be hated by all nations because of My name. 

After all these major disasters of nature there is still more to come, which is the persecution of Christians. This is a sign that we are not at that stage yet, for we are not yet hated by all nations. Even though many Christians are murdered every day, we are far from the height of persecution when we compare this to that of North Korea, China during the Communist Revolution and that which the early Christians suffered under Nero. Furthermore, not only the severity but also the spread is not all over the world. This is also because Christianity is not present yet all over the world in great numbers, which is also an indicator of the end of the end-time.
The fact that Jesus says, "**you will be hated by all nations"** also indicates that this is about Christians. Of course, Jews are hated everywhere as well, but they are not as present as Christians. For example, there are not many Jews in Indonesia, and so they are not much hated there, but Christians are hated there. Of course, the greatest proof of this is that Christ says **because of My name**, in this case the lowercase letter is correct, because He is speaking of His name, Jesus Christ[^2], and it is only Christians who bear His name in their identity. 
So in other words, we will know we are nearing the end-time when we send more missionaries to convert many more Christians across the world and when Christians, including ourselves, are being persecuted. #tags/evangelism/persecution At that time, however, there will be no organization to help out Christians such as *Open Doors*, for its current (mostly Western) members who support persecuted Christians all around the world, will themselves be persecuted, and no secular organization, such as #todo/opzoeken *Amnesty International*—which proved its bias during the war between Israel and Gaza #tags/history/war/israel/2023 —or the United Nations—which have always been partial and towards the destruction of Israel—will help out Christians.

> **10** “And at that time many will fall away and will betray one another and hate one another.

On the largest scale nations and countries will fight each other in the end-time, but on the smallest scale every person will fight one another, even within families, where a father will even turn against his own son #todo/opzoeken/reference. There will be no one who will trust another person anymore. In history this has been shown to happen when people do not feel secure. . 

> **11** “Many false prophets will arise and will deceive many. **12** “And because lawlessness is multiplied, most people’s love will grow cold. 

It is important to note that **because lawlessness is multiplied, most people's love will grow cold**. Where the world thinks that love expresses itself in sexual freedom and freedom from moral values which ultimately lead to adultery, sodomy (intercourse with people of the same sex) and all forms of sex outside of marriage #tags/sex/marriage/before, love actually becomes less. Any example of this will suffice, but let us take that of the movie... where the protagonists daughter has run away and turns out to have run from home because she did not feel that her father loves her, and became an adult movie star, simply to gain attention from men. In fact what happens to these type of women is that they actually become less able to feel love,  attraction and affection, either from men or from anyone else. A specific hormone in the human body (but also found in animals), oxytocin, makes one feel safe and beloved. However, people who have had multiple sexual partners are less able to create this hormone (or become less sensitive to it). #biblestudy/discovery

> **13** “But the one who endures to the end, he will be saved. **14** “And this gospel of the kingdom shall be proclaimed in the whole world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come.

This is one of the more important indicators of the end-time, because it gives a clue of when Jesus returns. However, since it is not clear what the **whole world** is, or what **all the nations** means, it does not help us in providing any details and every guess still remains but a speculation. First of all it is not clear whether all the nations literally refers to just that, in which case Christians  still have a long to go  #todo/nogaftemaken. Then, aside from the definition of nation there is also the definition of the whole world. Would it suffice for, say, a hundred persons of each nation to be reached before Jesus returns? And is that 100 persons of each tribe, or actually each nation? 
We must understand that Jesus statement is a *prerequisite* but not an *instruction* to hasten the coming of the Kingdom. God has His own time and does not need to tell man the exact time. If He wanted to, He would have let His Son know Who would have answered the disciples when they asked for it. 

> ### Abomination of Desolation and the Tribulation
> **Matt. 24:15**   “Therefore when you see the ABOMINATION OF DESOLATION which was spoken of through Daniel the prophet, standing in the holy place (let the reader understand), **16** then those who are in Judea must flee to the mountains. **17** “Whoever is on the housetop must not go down to get the things out that are in his house. **18** “And whoever is in the field must not turn back to get his garment. **19** “But woe to those who are pregnant and to those who are nursing babies in those days! **20** “But pray that your flight will not be in the winter, or on a Sabbath.

It is clear why it is not advised to be pregnant and nursing at the time of flight, or why it is disadvantageous of this occurs **in the winter**, because of food shortage among other reasons, and overnight stays in the wilderness, but would the Sabbath rule still apply in these conditions? Sabbath was made for man, and man should keep it to worship God and get rest for his body. Even during a flight from the Romans, would a day's rest not be a good thing? 
As for the latter question, yes, this would be a thing for the body and mind, to alleviate of one's stress and to obtain peace of mind by worshipping God. However, this will also mean that there is a bigger probability that one gets caught by the Romans because one is not moving. The longer one stays in Roman territory, and more specifically in Israel, the more dangerous it becomes. 
Another reason why one should pray that one's flight is not on a Sabbat is because if one decides to neglect the Sabbath and to continue fleeing on that day, he misses out on the worship of God, fellowship with his brothers and rest for his body and mind. This, though it seems small, is detrimental, especially in situations under pressure. Where the world thinks that an extra day of flight might provide one with a higher probability of survival, in the Kingdom it is so that the probability of surviving increases with the amount of time one spends with God[^3]. 
Take for example the prophet Daniel, humanly seen #questions/language/english/word, his survival rates were highest if he complied with the law and did not go against his enemies, but he chose to trust and obey God first, which caused him to be in trouble for a little while, e.g. to be thrown in the lion's den, but to be recovered far greatly than that after he had passed the test. Or bit more in recent history, let us look at Olympic Gold medallist Eric Liddell, who decided not to train or compete on Sundays because of the compulsory rest on the Lord's Day, and forwent his chances of winning an Olympic medal, received another opportunity to run, not the 100 meters, which he trained for, but the 200 and 400 meters, for which he received Bronze and Gold, respectively. Despite a day of less training, God gave him power to run an even longer distance for which he had not even trained.

> **21** “For then there will be a great tribulation, such as has not occurred since the beginning of the world until now, nor ever will. **22** “And unless those days had been cut short, no life would have been saved; but for the sake of the elect those days will be cut short. 

The **elect** are those who have not been reborn yet but have been chosen. God gives them time to repent and convert. This interpretation would apply both for those who adhere to the Pre- as well as the Post-Tribulation interpretation. 
If the **elect** are those already reborn, that is the believers, it will not matter whether **those days will be cut short**, for they will go to Heaven anyway. However, this will not fit the Pre-Tribulation interpretation because those who hold to this doctrine believe that believers will have been taken up to Heaven before the Great Tribulation #tags/theology/eschatology/rapture. 

We cannot imagine what the Great Tribulation will be like #tags/theology/eschatology/tribulation. It **has not occurred since the beginning of the world** so it is nothing like the Big Bang, the disasters of Noah's Flood or any of the wars, however gruesome they were. Even the Assyrian extermination of whole populations was still in the order of single digit percentages of the population of the world. In the Tribulation two-digit percentages are not unimaginable. There will be great losses all over the world. The difference with natural disasters in the last centuries is that those occurred in isolation, except for earthquakes which go together with tsunamis. In the Tribulation, however, many natural disasters will occur *simultaneously* and *all over the world* so that there is no place to escape. And where there are no disasters the *people* will be killing and betraying each other, being misled by the Anti-Christ. 


> **23** “Then if anyone says to you, ‘Behold, here is the Christ,’ or ‘There *He is*,’ do not believe *him*. **24** “For false christs and false prophets will arise and will show great signs and wonders, so as to deceive, if possible, even the elect.

The encouraging message Jesus gives here is that it is not possible to deceive the elect. Again, there are two interpretations of the elect, the first is those who have been chosen but have not converted yet, and the second one is those who have already converted. In both cases the elect will not be deceived by the **false christs** because they will be kept safe, either by cutting the time short of the Great Tribulation, or by preventing the elect from meeting with the false christs. If it concerns believers, then perhaps these will even die before they meet or are convinced by any Anti-Christ. But if concerns elected unbelievers, the only way for them to not be deceived by an Anti-Christ and live through the Tribulation as unbelievers is if they never meet the Anti-Christ or become believers before that. 
Personally, I have some difficulties with this interpretation because limits will be placed on how things must happen, while God has said that His ways are higher than ours #todo/opzoeken/reference and hence He can do things we cannot imagine. #todo/nogaftemaken misschien een tekening toevoegen om het duidelijker te maken. 

> **25** “Behold, I have told you in advance. **26** “Therefore, if they say to you, ‘Behold, He is in the wilderness,’ do not go out, *or*, ‘Behold, He is in the inner rooms,’ do not believe *them*. **27** “For just as the lightning comes from the east and appears even to the west, so will the coming of the Son of Man be. **28** “Wherever the corpse is, there the vultures will gather.

No one has to worry about missing the coming of the Son of Man. Everyone will see it, wherever they live. To extend the analogy of the **lightning**, the **coming of the Son of Man** will even be visible to those living in underground bunkers, in submarines and even in outer space and in other galaxies. 
The point Jesus is making here is that we do not need to guess when He is coming back

> ### The Coming of the Son of Man
> **Matt. 24:29**   “But immediately after the tribulation of those days THE SUN WILL BE DARKENED, AND THE MOON WILL NOT GIVE ITS LIGHT, AND THE STARS WILL FALL from the sky, and the POWERS OF THE HEAVENS will be shaken.

There are many theories on how the end will come about, but none of those theories can explain why the stars will not shine anymore. Of course, a volcanic eruption might cloud the sky for a while so that neither the sun, moon nor stars are visible—which happened during the Flood and caused the great ice age—but that would only be temporary and has never occurred worldwide. 

> **30** “And then the sign of the Son of Man will appear in the sky, and then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the SON OF MAN COMING ON THE CLOUDS OF THE SKY with power and great glory. **31** “And He will send forth His angels with A GREAT TRUMPET and THEY WILL GATHER TOGETHER His elect from the four winds, from one end of the sky to the other.

The meaning of **elect** here is not interpretable in two ways. The most common understanding is that these are Christians who have endured through the Tribulation. Some may have come to faith only during that period, yet both the Pre-Tribulation and Post-Tribulation perspectives agree that at this point they are true believers in Christ.
A less common interpretation holds that not all of these elect are yet believers. According to this view, some are chosen by God but will only come to faith afterwards—in the millennial kingdom that follows the Tribulation. All of these interpretations presuppose a distinctly Premillennial framework.

> ### The Parable of the Fig Tree
> **Matt. 24:32**   “Now learn the parable from the fig tree: when its branch has already become tender and puts forth its leaves, you know that summer is near; **33** so, you too, when you see all these things, recognize that He is near, *right* at the door. **34** “Truly I say to you, this generation will not pass away until all these things take place. **35** “Heaven and earth will pass away, but My words will not pass away.

If we take Jesus' words at face value, then we will know the end is near when we see wars and nations raging against one another. This is not the case yet, so we are far from the end, we are not even in the Tribulation yet. However, the Tribulation could very well start tomorrow, all of a sudden, through a worldwide calamity which causes all nations and people to go against each other due to a shortage of food. 

> **Matt. 24:36**   “But of that day and hour no one knows, not even the angels of heaven, nor the Son, but the Father alone. 

One argument by proponents of the Pre- Tribulation—against the Post-Tribulation perspective—is that this verse says that no one knows the day and hour, but because it is known that the Tribulation lasts seven years (see Daniel 9 #todo/opzoeken/reference) it would be known when Jesus comes. There are two main counterarguments. The first is that the Tribulation does not exactly have to be seven years. Even if it exactly has this duration, it is not certain from when to start counting. When exactly, for example, would be the day of the coming of the Anti-Christ? 
The second counterargument is that if the Second Coming of Christ comes before the Tribulation and its time is unknown, then the coming of Christ after the Tribulation would be the Third Coming, but no such thing is mentioned in the Bible. The proponents argue then that neither Christ's coming before or after the Tribulation are the Second Coming but both of these *together* are #todo/opzoeken —together they are *one coming* divided in two stages, first for the Church, then for those who convert  during the Tribulation, that is, the elect as in the verses above. 

> **37** “For just as the days of Noah were, so the coming of the Son of Man will be. **38** “For as in those days before the flood they were eating and drinking, marrying and giving in marriage, until the day that Noah entered the ark, **39** and they did not understand until the flood came and took them all away; so will the coming of the Son of Man be. 

The people in Noah's time knew nothing about what was going to happen to them. Even up until **the day that Noah entered the Ark**, even **until the flood came**, minutes or hours later, they did not know! Perhaps they did not even understand until the flood **took them all away**. Whereas Christians recognize the signs of the end even in this day, it is of no use to tell the world about this. If they will not even realize the danger they are in up to the minute *before the Tribulation*, how will they possibly understand right now, years before it. 
Through God's mercy the world does have another chance and that is that when the Tribulation starts they might be able to realize the truth, and in the first 3.5 years of the Tribulation the signs will not be as severe as as in the last 3.5 years, so that many are still alive and able to change their lives and commit themselves to Christ. 

> **40** “Then there will be two in the field; one will be taken, and one will be left. **41** “Two women *will be* grinding *grain* at the mill; one will be taken and one will be left.

This imagery plays an important part in the believers of the Pre-Tribulation and many movies have depicted this, even one with Nicolas Cage #tags/people/actors (who is not a Christian at all) and Kevin Sorbo #tags/people/christian/actors#, such as in the movie #todo/opzoeken #tags/movies/christian *Taken Away*. However, most likely the imagery is not what Jesus meant. It is simply a way of saying that one will belong to the Lord and the other will not. Jesus' words can *not* be taken at face value here, for example, we can not interpret this as a fifty-fifty ratio between believers and unbelievers. In the same way we should not literally interpret a person as being **taken**. Neither should the following verses be taken literally, but see notes there.

> ### Be Ready for His Coming
> **Matt. 24:42**   “Therefore stay awake, for you do not know which day your Lord is coming. **43** “But be sure of this, that if the head of the house had known at what time of the night the thief was coming, he would have stayed awake and would not have allowed his house to be broken into. **44** “For this reason you also must be ready; for the Son of Man is coming at an hour when you do not think *He will*.

This, too, should not be taken literally as in staying **awake** all the time. Jesus' coming will be unexpected and some will literally be asleep at that time and wake up, but the main message to Christians is that no Christian should think he can freely sin and ask for forgiveness and better his life just before Jesus' coming, so that he can enjoy his sinful life as long as possible. 

> **Matt. 24:45**   “Who then is the faithful and prudent slave whom his master put in charge of his household to give them their food at the proper time? **46** “Blessed is that slave whom his master finds so doing when he comes. **47** “Truly I say to you that he will put him in charge of all his possessions.

Even though the analogy given here is in the context of the Second Coming, there will be a big reward for the believer who shows himself to be responsible, both in this world as well as in the next, and terrible punishment for those who abuse the freedom and power given them by God  

> **48** “But if that evil slave says in his heart, ‘My master is not coming for a long time,’ **49** and begins to beat his fellow slaves and eat and drink with drunkards; **50** the master of that slave will come on a day when he does not expect *him* and at an hour which he does not know, **51** and will cut him in pieces and assign him a place with the hypocrites; in that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.

In the end, we are all slaves but we can be a master of slaves when God has given us that position or a lower slave. The master slaves, however, bear more responsibility will be punished more greatly when he fails at his task. This principle is also found in 1 Corinthians when Paul speaks of the position of the elders, but can in fact be applied anywhere, even to parents. Those who have the blessing of becoming a parent will also have a higher responsibility in taking care of this creation of God and bringing it to Christ. So God's first command to Adam to multiply and populate the Earth was a difficult one. It is hard enough to raise one child to even become well-behaved and walking on the right path, let alone bring him to Christ. How much more difficult will it be to do this for multiple children?
